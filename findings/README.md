# Completed deductions and their useful consequences

These results extend the earlier research's specific cases. They are an entrance for questions that the available premises can settle. They do not decide which new questions, aims, or standards may become worth pursuing. The [25 inquiries](../inquiries/perspective-25/README.md), [contributor inventory](../mind-change/targets.md), and [change map](../changes/README.md) retain their different roles.

| Question | Completed result | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| When can a recipe compensate for its executor? | [Common-action intersection theorem and finite optimal question policy](recipe-adequacy.md) | Detect an information collision, construct a sufficient question, or return an exact unresolved witness. |
| Can the goal or question itself change? | [Exploration, execution, and criterion revision separated](exploration-and-goal-change.md) | Explore before fixing the destination; keep earlier comparisons under their earlier criteria. |
| Which representation preserves the answer? | [Necessary and sufficient answer-preservation condition](organization-and-representation.md) | Preserve the query-relevant distinctions and their reconstruction paths. |
| What does incomplete evidence already settle? | [Full binary signal family and exact minimax procedure](information-and-action.md) | Compute conditional answers, including cases where the old policy deletion reverses. |
| What happens when assumptions change? | [Partial dependence, timing, fee, and control-cost thresholds](dependence-and-changed-models.md) | Use exact ranges and policy boundaries instead of postponing every changed parameter. |
| How do several failures or improvements combine? | [Minimal repairs and composition countermodels](composition-and-failure.md) | Repair the actual mixed mechanism; avoid assuming individual benefit implies combined benefit. |
| Can timing reverse feasibility? | [Anticipatory switching and total reuse cost](time-effort-and-value.md) | Find an omitted feasible route and count displaced effort. |
| Which neighbor-XOR rings erase all states? | [Power-of-two classification and tight n-step bound](neighbor-xor.md) | Answer the extinction question for every positive width. |
