# Beneficial mind change

A mind can change in what is noticed, experienced, accessible, understood, wanted, permitted, remembered, imagined, coordinated, or possible to do. Belief revision is one part of this scope. A change in the relation between existing contents can matter even when no new proposition is acquired.

The project’s governing aim is to make valuable changes available and produce them as beneficially, quickly, and efficiently as the situation permits. The research and its presentation are themselves occasions for such change. A visitor should encounter the distinction, connection, operation, experience, or support that advances their present situation.

The beneficiary and relevant improvement determine what “beneficial” means in a particular use. Direction, extent, onset, duration, side effects, transfer, and resource demands can differ. Greater confidence may help appropriate action or entrench an error. Less attention may remove a distraction or conceal a necessary signal. Retaining a warranted judgment, preventing an unwanted change, or making an already understood idea govern action can be the useful result.

Immediate value should begin as early as possible. Felt impact, speed of reading, durable improvement, and effort imposed on the recipient are distinct considerations. A quick contribution that removes an obstacle can also enable a slower change. No arbitrary scalar score or requirement to maximize the number of changed targets is implied.

“Direct contributor” identifies a nearby input, operation, connection, enabling condition, or obstruction at the specified functional or experiential level. The target inventory supplies candidate relationships. A particular recipe must establish which relationship matters here and supply the actual operation needed to use it.

The current context determines the missing contribution. If the user already understands the broad scope, explaining that scope again does not supply the requested contributors. If a procedure is already known but unavailable when needed, another explanation can leave access unchanged. If the goal itself is unsettled, requiring a frozen target before inquiry can obstruct the work that would establish one.

Changes in an AI’s working interpretation, accessible records, chosen operations, generated code, and supporting organization can be observed at their own scopes. These do not establish human feelings, changed model weights, or reliable persistence across future sessions.
