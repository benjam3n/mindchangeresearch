# Contribution construction

A useful recipe supplies the operation that makes the intended change possible in specified conditions. Its user should not have to invent that operation while following the recipe. A target, a contributor list, a selection card, and a completed recipe are distinct objects.

The present context can already supply the target, mechanism, or usable result. Begin with the live missing contribution. Diagnosis is needed where materially different explanations require different operations; it is not a compulsory opening for a settled case. Exploration can construct the target or criterion. Retention can make a previously established result operative without rediscovery.

| Available situation | Required construction | Result that can be used |
|---|---|---|
| A distinction is absent | Find cases requiring different treatment; expose the difference that changes the treatment | A discriminating relation with its application condition |
| Contents exist separately | Identify what one permits, constrains, explains, or supplies to the other; perform that connection | A usable implication, dependency, or composition |
| A result is known but inaccessible | Link a detectable use condition to its canonical record and needed operation | Retrieval that returns the result together with its conditions |
| A crucial operation is missing | Supply its transformation through a worked performance, code, tool, demonstration, or decomposed executable components | An operation the specified executor can actually perform |
| An approach is available but irrelevant | Locate the consequential difference between its conditions and the live case | Reuse, adaptation, approach creation, or an exact remaining gap |
| An experiential contributor is missing | Distinguish the relevant experience from its expression, appraisal, willingness, and actual control; supply the available contributor | A real contribution at its available scope; recipient effect remains separately observed |
| A correction has been acknowledged but does not govern conduct | Bind its trigger to a replacement operation and update the actual consumer | Changed dependent output or conduct |
| The target or standard is unsettled | Construct contrasts that reveal different worthwhile continuations | A revised question, criterion, concern, or opportunity |

These are conditional construction families, not an exhaustive dispatcher. The [target-specific contributors](targets.md) supply additional candidates. The [required systems](subject-systems.md) govern the relevant assistant weaknesses.

## Consequential distinction

**Condition:** the live task treats two cases as equivalent although a required operation must differ.

**Inputs:** the cases, the task’s actual requirement, and what the recipient already distinguishes.

**Operations:** put the cases in the same representation; hold their shared relevant features explicit; identify a feature that changes a required inference or action; derive the different treatment; give the distinction a name only if the name supports reuse. If no treatment or experience differs, the proposed contrast has not established a consequential distinction.

**Worked use:** a required condition is `False` in case A and `None` in case B. Python truthiness sends both through the same false branch. Under the source contract A is an observed failure; B is unknown. Preserve three states with `{True: 'true', False: 'false', None: 'unknown'}` before calling the receiving gate. The [54-case probe](../sources/chat-20260908/audit_results.json) records 18 wrong statuses with truthiness and 54 exact statuses with this mapping. Unknown is now available to the receiving operation instead of disappearing during conversion.

**Boundary:** when the source already uses the receiving contract’s exact values, conversion is unnecessary. Status equality alone does not establish preservation of optional observations or activity state.

## Consequence construction

**Condition:** a premise is already accepted and the next useful implication has not been supplied.

**Inputs:** the accepted premise, a receiving operation, existing consequences, and any unresolved assumptions.

**Operations:** identify what the receiving operation would need to do differently if the premise is taken seriously; write the intermediate relation that licenses that difference; resolve or retain each added assumption; produce the changed object or operation when authorized. Stop repeating the premise once it ceases to supply a missing relation.

**Worked use:** the accepted premise is that organization should efficiently produce beneficial mind change. The current repository hides 35 distinct index targets inside an archive. Archive recovery adds a prerequisite to using those findings. Therefore the current view must expose the target, its usable contributor, conditions, and provenance at a direct path. This integration creates that view and publishes every local destination it links. The observable result is removal of the archive-extraction prerequisite; a human speed or benefit effect requires separate observation.

**Boundary:** a source-fidelity audit may appropriately begin with original source order. Direct subject access does not eliminate that alternative use.

## Availability and retrieval

**Condition:** the required material exists but its current access route does not return it with the conditions needed for use.

**Inputs:** a stable target identity, canonical content, use condition, and the consuming operation.

**Operations:** retain one canonical target record; associate its identity and relevant terms with a direct path; return its definition, contributors, conditions, and evidence standing together; pass those fields to the current construction. If the target is absent, return absence rather than a neighboring record as an answer.

**Executable use:** from the repository root, `python3 tools/mind_change.py --target 368 --json` returns initiation and its contributors; `python3 tools/mind_change.py --target AI-1 --json` returns request-interpretation revision. `--search 'retrieval cue'` returns records containing both terms. This is literal retrieval, not a claim of semantic diagnosis or of causing initiation.

**Boundary:** retrieving a method does not show that its conditions hold, the executor can perform it, or the recipient has learned it.

## Capability construction

**Condition:** a procedure contains an operation the intended executor cannot yet perform or an unspecified transformation that permits incompatible outputs.

**Inputs:** an actual input, required result, available executor capabilities, and the first unresolved transformation.

**Operations:** perform the transformation on the input; expose the objects consumed and produced; construct a second case where a superficial imitation would fail; retain the condition that selects the right operation; supply executable code, a worked performance, an available tool, or an exact dependency for the still unavailable component. Reinspect the next step, because repairing one missing operation can expose another.

**Worked use:** the former recipe compiler says “select a capable mechanism,” but that phrase cannot construct a new mechanism for every target. Its historical specificity record therefore cannot pass an implementation-completeness check. The current recipe families supply operations for distinction, consequence, retrieval, and correction; the 616 target entries remain candidate maps until the relevant transformation is supplied. The [historical interface probe](../sources/chat-20260908/audit_rsi.py) supplies actual code for one finite transformation rather than a universal mechanism.

**Boundary:** a worked example can make supported execution available while independent performance, retention, and transfer remain open. An unavailable physical action must retain its specific dependency; the research should still construct the useful available components.

## Experience and agency

**Condition:** the sought change concerns emotion, motivation, felt possibility, willingness, inhibition, or agency.

**Inputs:** recipient information actually available, the specified component, its candidate contributor, and relevant available actions or interaction.

**Operations:** distinguish felt experience from expressed agreement, appraisal, willingness, ability, and actual influence; identify the proposed relationship from the relevant target entry; supply the available contribution in the appropriate form; preserve the recipient’s ability to accept, reject, or alter it; keep actual recipient effects separate from the act of supplying it.

**Worked construction:** if the established obstacle is lack of influence over a shared plan, identify which choices the person actually owns and change the plan or interface to expose those choices. If the choices cannot be provided, preserve that constraint rather than describing the person as newly empowered. This supplies an agency-related condition when performed; it does not establish a felt effect from wording alone.

**Boundary:** the same expressed hesitation can arise from different circumstances. Do not invent fear, low motivation, incapacity, or a hidden value conflict when the context has not supplied them.

## Correction retention

**Condition:** a specific recurring mismatch and its replacement are established.

**Inputs:** trigger, scope, replacement operation, rationale, and every active consumer that can reproduce the mismatch.

**Operations:** locate the consumer; replace the operation at its source; retain the correction and its applicable conditions; run the relevant next use; inspect the produced behavior; preserve a boundary where the former form remains appropriate. A promise or stored instruction alone does not complete this construction.

**Worked use:** the old checkpoint script regenerated a status-first index and converted repository-relative links into transient `sandbox:` links. This integration removes that generator path, generates accounting in `research/status.md`, leaves the substantive index intact, and packages all linked current material. Running the checkpoint generator and checking the resulting index is the relevant next use.

**Boundary:** status-first output remains appropriate when the user asks for exact progress. The correction concerns the project’s default substantive access, not a prohibition on accounting.

## Recipe completeness

A recipe is incomplete if two executions can obey its stated instructions yet choose incompatible transformations because a consequential selection or construction rule is missing. A named system can supply that rule only when its operations or an applicable retained result are actually used.

Each particular recipe retains its target, starting conditions, required inputs, available executor, executable operations, expected result, important boundaries, and relevant evidence. The number of filled fields is not the completeness criterion. Simple uses need not pay the cost of a full research record. Human change, durable transfer, and comparative efficiency are credited only at the scope actually supported.
