# Recursive improvement

Subject Systems can supply distinctions and operations for a relevant subject. Perspective optimization can change what is noticed, how a matter is interpreted, which result is worth pursuing, and which operations become relevant. The relationship is recursive when improvements change the conditions and operations that produce later improvements.

A useful result becomes part of that process through an identifiable consumer: an application condition retrieves it, a selection uses its distinction, a construction performs its operation, a generator expresses it, or a revision changes dependent work. Storage supplies persistence; an actual later use supplies different evidence. Increasing a catalog or declaring a transition does not itself establish either improved performance or a usable improvement mechanism.

| Required contribution when absent | What must become available | Scope of evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Target relevance | The change needed in the current situation, including a revised goal, criterion, question, or opportunity | A consequential interpretation and its actual use |
| Mechanism construction | An operation capable of supplying the missing contributor | Worked or executable transformation with its conditions |
| Composition | Outputs preserve the meanings required by their consumers, including unknown and unavailable states | Contract-preserving use across the relevant interface |
| Correction uptake | The cause of a recurring error is revised in the actual consumer | Changed dependent conduct or output |
| Retention | Trigger, operation, scope, reason, and provenance remain retrievable | A later relevant use rather than preservation alone |
| Generalization | The improvement remains useful where consequential conditions vary | Further applicable cases and retained failures; no universal extrapolation |
| Improvement of improvement | The retained change improves a subsequent selection, construction, correction, or reuse process | A trace linking the earlier result to the later operation and its consequence |

These are possible missing contributions, not a fixed stage sequence. A useful existing result can enter directly; a new distinction or mechanism can require construction. An already settled matter need not be rediscovered through trial and error.
