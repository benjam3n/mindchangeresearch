# Representation

Candidate contributors describe possible functional relationships. Each source marker supports only its attached statement at the [specified scope](../evidence.md). [Construction](../contribution-construction.md) supplies operations for a particular use.

<a id="target-107"></a>
## 107. Verbal representation

expressing a matter in usable words.

**Candidate contributors:** Naming entities and relations; constructing a concrete sentence; resolving ambiguous referents; supplying words for the missing distinction.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Fluent wording can conceal unresolved content; verbal availability is not evidence of truth.

<a id="target-108"></a>
## 108. Visual representation

making an image or diagram available.

**Candidate contributors:** A diagram or image with meaningful correspondence; contrastive visual examples; spatially displaying otherwise hidden relations.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Visual detail can imply unsupported precision or causal links; correspondence must remain inspectable.

<a id="target-109"></a>
## 109. Spatial representation

organizing material through positions or distances.

**Candidate contributors:** Assigning stable locations; mapping distance or adjacency to a specified relationship; arranging objects to expose comparisons.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Spatial distance must mean something defined; arbitrary placement can suggest a false relation.

<a id="target-110"></a>
## 110. Quantitative representation

expressing magnitudes and relationships numerically.

**Candidate contributors:** Measurement; explicit units and denominators; counting comparable cases; representing uncertainty or scale numerically.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Numbers without justified measurement rules create precision without a valid quantity.

<a id="target-111"></a>
## 111. Procedural representation

organizing material as something to do.

**Candidate contributors:** Identifying inputs, operations, order, and completion conditions; a worked execution; a branch for a consequential alternative.

**Conditions and distinctions:** A procedure is usable only when the actor can perform its steps with available inputs.

<a id="target-112"></a>
## 112. Narrative representation

organizing events through a story.

**Candidate contributors:** Temporal order; agents and aims; causally connected events; selecting a viewpoint and relevant turning points.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Narrative coherence can invite causal conclusions beyond the evidence.

<a id="target-113"></a>
## 113. Structural representation

exposing parts, relations, and dependencies.

**Candidate contributors:** Making components and dependencies explicit; distinguishing levels; tracing what consumes each intermediate result.

**Conditions and distinctions:** A diagram of boxes does not establish the proposed dependencies or their sufficiency.

<a id="target-114"></a>
## 114. Compression

reducing representational size while preserving needed distinctions.

**Candidate contributors:** Removing repetition; substituting known chunks; preserving decision-relevant differences; deleting details irrelevant to the intended use.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Compression must specify its consumer; lossless for one use can be destructive for another.

<a id="target-115"></a>
## 115. Expansion

making previously compressed distinctions explicit.

**Candidate contributors:** Unpacking an abbreviation; restoring omitted premises or cases; expanding a formula into operations; exposing hidden choices.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Expansion helps when it supplies missing distinctions; extra wording can increase load without doing so.

<a id="target-116"></a>
## 116. Externalization

using an external representation to alter what is mentally available.

**Candidate contributors:** Writing, diagramming, arranging, or computing a representation that can be consulted and manipulated.

**Conditions and distinctions:** The person must interpret and coordinate with the aid; external form can change task difficulty [S10](../evidence.md#s10).
