# Meaning and purpose

Candidate contributors describe possible functional relationships. Each source marker supports only its attached statement at the [specified scope](../evidence.md). [Construction](../contribution-construction.md) supplies operations for a particular use.

<a id="target-453"></a>
## 453. Significance

something coming to seem meaningful or meaningless.

**Candidate contributors:** Connection to a valued concern; participation in a larger activity or relationship; a newly recognized consequence; loss of an old organizing relation.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Meaning can change while the immediate task stays identical; the changed relation should be specified.

<a id="target-454"></a>
## 454. Purpose formation

adopting a reason that organizes continuing activity.

**Candidate contributors:** A concern able to organize repeated choices; an adopted contribution or responsibility; concrete occasions for enactment.

**Conditions and distinctions:** A purpose statement becomes operative only through its relationship to actual allocation and action.

<a id="target-455"></a>
## 455. Coherence

experiencing matters as fitting together or fragmenting.

**Candidate contributors:** Recognizable relationships among events and commitments; resolution of contradictions; a narrative or model connecting previously fragmented material.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Felt coherence can increase through an inaccurate simplifying account.

<a id="target-456"></a>
## 456. Direction

gaining or losing a sense of where activity is leading.

**Candidate contributors:** An intelligible next dependency; a credible route from current activity to a valued outcome; visible cumulative progress.

**Conditions and distinctions:** A detailed schedule may create direction without establishing that the destination is worthwhile.

<a id="target-457"></a>
## 457. Contribution meaning

recognizing how one's activity matters beyond its immediate performance.

**Candidate contributors:** Seeing who uses the result; observing consequences beyond the task; feedback from beneficiaries; connecting local work to a larger dependency.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Symbolic recognition and actual contribution can differ; both should remain identifiable.

<a id="target-458"></a>
## 458. Existential orientation

changing one's relation to uncertainty, finitude, or life's possibilities.

**Candidate contributors:** Reflection on uncertainty or finitude; encounters with loss or possibility; philosophical or spiritual interpretations; chosen commitments under unresolved questions.

**Conditions and distinctions:** No single framing is guaranteed to fit every person's worldview or experience.

<a id="target-459"></a>
## 459. Purpose revision

replacing an organizing commitment that no longer fits.

**Candidate contributors:** A persistent mismatch with lived concerns; changed conditions or capacities; an alternative organizing commitment; permission to release obsolete obligations.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Replacing purpose can require transition support; a new slogan may leave prior allocation intact.
