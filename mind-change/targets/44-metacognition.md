# Metacognition

Candidate contributors describe possible functional relationships. Each source marker supports only its attached statement at the [specified scope](../evidence.md). [Construction](../contribution-construction.md) supplies operations for a particular use.

<a id="target-416"></a>
## 416. Knowledge monitoring

recognizing what one knows and does not know.

**Candidate contributors:** Attempted retrieval; source and scope checks; a question requiring the allegedly known information; comparison with an authoritative record.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Failure to retrieve can reflect access difficulty rather than absent learning; successful guessing can mislead too.

<a id="target-417"></a>
## 417. Comprehension monitoring

distinguishing understanding from familiarity with wording.

**Candidate contributors:** Explaining the mechanism; deriving a consequence; applying it to a new case; locating missing dependencies.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Self-rated understanding may fall after explanatory demands expose gaps [S14](../evidence.md#s14); an exposed gap still needs repair.

<a id="target-418"></a>
## 418. Error detection

noticing a mistake in one's own activity.

**Candidate contributors:** A discrepancy between intended and observed result; an invariant check; a counterexample; an independently constructed comparison.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Repeated inspection using the same mistaken assumption can miss the same error.

<a id="target-419"></a>
## 419. Assumption detection

recognizing an unexamined starting commitment.

**Candidate contributors:** Making premises explicit; varying a supposedly fixed condition; asking what a conclusion requires; a case where the starting commitment fails.

**Conditions and distinctions:** An assumption can be useful when openly provisional; detection does not require immediate rejection.

<a id="target-420"></a>
## 420. Conflict detection

recognizing incompatible demands or interpretations.

**Candidate contributors:** Making competing demands simultaneously available; aligning their scope; constructing a case where they prescribe incompatible continuations.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Conflicting wording can be resolved by interpretation, while conflicting requirements may need a substantive choice.

<a id="target-421"></a>
## 421. Method applicability

recognizing whether a procedure fits the present matter.

**Candidate contributors:** Comparing required inputs and exclusions with the live case; an applicable and inapplicable example; inspection of the method's actual output.

**Conditions and distinctions:** A familiar method's name or subject area does not establish applicability.

<a id="target-422"></a>
## 422. Mental-state recognition

noticing that fatigue, emotion, or attention is affecting activity.

**Candidate contributors:** A change in performance or experience; an independent state cue; comparison across conditions; specific recognition of distraction or fatigue.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Subjective adaptation can conceal performance impairment; one feeling is not a complete state measure [S01](../evidence.md#s01).

<a id="target-423"></a>
## 423. Confidence monitoring

noticing mismatch between certainty and support.

**Candidate contributors:** A confident claim with weak grounds; a low-confidence answer with strong evidence; resolved predictions; separation of familiarity from support.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Monitoring a mismatch is different from correcting the cue that produced it.

<a id="target-424"></a>
## 424. Limitation localization

identifying the particular operation that is unavailable.

**Candidate contributors:** Tracing failure to a missing distinction, input, operation, resource, or interface; preserving components that still work.

**Conditions and distinctions:** A global label such as 'bad reasoning' does not locate an actionable limitation.

<a id="target-425"></a>
## 425. Self-correction

changing one's activity in response to a recognized inadequacy.

**Candidate contributors:** A recognized error connected to a replacement operation; revision of dependent work; later access to the correction at its trigger.

**Conditions and distinctions:** An acknowledgment without changed applicable conduct leaves correction uptake unestablished.
