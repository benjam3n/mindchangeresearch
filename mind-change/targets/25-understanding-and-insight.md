# Understanding and insight

Candidate contributors describe possible functional relationships. Each source marker supports only its attached statement at the [specified scope](../evidence.md). [Construction](../contribution-construction.md) supplies operations for a particular use.

<a id="target-222"></a>
## 222. Comprehension

making sense of previously opaque material.

**Candidate contributors:** Resolving unknown terms; a concrete example; showing the governing relation; supplying a missing prerequisite; reducing irrelevant representational load.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Smooth reading is insufficient evidence of comprehension; the needed use determines what must become available.

<a id="target-223"></a>
## 223. Integration

understanding how formerly separate pieces fit together.

**Candidate contributors:** Mapping correspondences between pieces; resolving incompatible assumptions; showing their shared dependency; constructing a combined example.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Combining statements without reconciling their conditions can create superficial coherence.

<a id="target-224"></a>
## 224. Consequence access

becoming able to derive what an account implies.

**Candidate contributors:** Making premises available; performing the missing inference; a contrast revealing what changes downstream; carrying qualifications into the result.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Restating the premise leaves consequence access unchanged when the recipient already knows it.

<a id="target-225"></a>
## 225. Operational understanding

becoming able to use an account in an activity.

**Candidate contributors:** A worked application; executable steps; required resources; an attempt on a relevant case; correction at the failed operation.

**Conditions and distinctions:** A description of use does not supply every motor, social, or cognitive ability needed to perform it.

<a id="target-226"></a>
## 226. Boundary understanding

recognizing when an account does not apply.

**Candidate contributors:** An inapplicable near neighbor; a violated precondition; an exception; comparison of successful and unsuccessful uses.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Knowing an example's failure does not identify the boundary unless the relevant difference is isolated.

<a id="target-227"></a>
## 227. Insight

reorganizing a matter so a previously inaccessible relationship becomes apparent.

**Candidate contributors:** A representation exposing an overlooked relation; abandoning a blocking assumption; joining previously separated information; a separating case.

**Conditions and distinctions:** The felt suddenness of insight does not establish accuracy or explain how the reorganization occurred.

<a id="target-228"></a>
## 228. Illusion of understanding

becoming confident in understanding without the corresponding ability.

**Candidate contributors:** Fluent explanations; familiar terms; repeated exposure; a persuasive narrative; absence of a demand to reconstruct missing steps.

**Conditions and distinctions:** These are possible sources of misplaced confidence; feeling able to explain can exceed actual explanatory performance [S14](../evidence.md#s14).

<a id="target-229"></a>
## 229. Understanding correction

recognizing and repairing that mismatch.

**Candidate contributors:** Attempting the required explanation or operation; locating the missing step; supplying its relation or input; reconstructing the account afterward.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Exposing a gap reduces an illusion only if the person recognizes the mismatch; repair is additional work [S14](../evidence.md#s14).

<a id="target-230"></a>
## 230. Explanatory independence

becoming able to reconstruct an understanding without repeating its wording.

**Candidate contributors:** Rebuilding from premises; expressing the relation in another form; producing a fresh example; answering a novel consequence question.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Paraphrase alone may preserve memorized wording without independent reconstruction.
