# Thinking strategy and intellectual stance

Candidate contributors describe possible functional relationships. Each source marker supports only its attached statement at the [specified scope](../evidence.md). [Construction](../contribution-construction.md) supplies operations for a particular use.

<a id="target-426"></a>
## 426. Approach selection

adopting a different way of proceeding.

**Candidate contributors:** The missing contribution; available methods with explicit outputs; a separating comparison; a new condition defeating the old approach.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Choosing a method and obtaining its promised result are separate events.

<a id="target-427"></a>
## 427. Approach construction

creating a method absent from the repertoire.

**Candidate contributors:** Decomposing the required result into operations; composing available capabilities; defining previously unspecified transitions; constructing a usable interface.

**Conditions and distinctions:** A description can hide unavailable suboperations; construction must expose those dependencies.

<a id="target-428"></a>
## 428. Question order

changing which determination is made first.

**Candidate contributors:** Actual prerequisites among determinations; availability of discriminating information; the cost of resolving a question too early.

**Conditions and distinctions:** A fixed universal question order can obstruct tasks whose useful entry point differs.

<a id="target-429"></a>
## 429. Depth

pursuing a relationship further before proceeding.

**Candidate contributors:** An unresolved consequential relation; a missing intermediate step; an explanation still relying on an unexamined dependency.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Additional depth helps only while it changes what the activity can establish or do.

<a id="target-430"></a>
## 430. Breadth

considering a wider range of matters or alternatives.

**Candidate contributors:** An excluded relevant domain; alternative mechanisms or formulations; variation in consequential dimensions; discovery of omitted cases.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Wider enumeration can add duplicates without improving coverage.

<a id="target-431"></a>
## 431. Explicitness

bringing an implicit operation into inspectable form.

**Candidate contributors:** Externalizing intermediate results, assumptions, selection criteria, or transformations; identifying the consumer of each.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Inspectability can improve without the underlying operation becoming correct.

<a id="target-432"></a>
## 432. Provisionality

separating temporary use from settled reliance.

**Candidate contributors:** Explicit status labels; preserving alternative accounts; limiting use to the assumed scope; a trigger for reconsideration.

**Conditions and distinctions:** A provisional premise must retain its status in downstream conclusions and later reuse.

<a id="target-433"></a>
## 433. Burden of justification

changing what must be established before accepting or excluding something.

**Candidate contributors:** Consequences of acceptance or exclusion; asymmetry of error; available support; the scope and strength of the claim.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Excessive demands can exclude useful justified possibilities; weak demands can admit unsupported ones.

<a id="target-434"></a>
## 434. Stopping rule

changing what warrants ending inquiry.

**Candidate contributors:** Completion conditions tied to the actual use; remaining consequential uncertainty; additional inquiry cost; a warranted continuation outside the current scope.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Stopping inquiry is not equivalent to declaring every parent question resolved.

<a id="target-435"></a>
## 435. Perspective replacement

changing the interpretation governing the activity.

**Candidate contributors:** A representation exposing an important omission; a better-supported interpretation; a changed target or criterion; reconstruction of dependent operations.

**Conditions and distinctions:** New language alone leaves the governing perspective unchanged if it selects the same inadequate continuation.
