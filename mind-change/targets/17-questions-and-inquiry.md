# Questions and inquiry

Candidate contributors describe possible functional relationships. Each source marker supports only its attached statement at the [specified scope](../evidence.md). [Construction](../contribution-construction.md) supplies operations for a particular use.

<a id="target-147"></a>
## 147. Question formation

becoming able to ask a previously unavailable question.

**Candidate contributors:** A newly available distinction; a contradiction; an unexplained contrast; a goal requiring an undetermined input.

**Conditions and distinctions:** A grammatically formed question may still have no determinate target or answer condition.

<a id="target-148"></a>
## 148. Gap recognition

identifying what remains undetermined.

**Candidate contributors:** Comparing available information with what an operation requires; naming an unresolved variable, alternative, relation, or boundary.

**Conditions and distinctions:** A generic admission of uncertainty does not identify the missing contribution.

<a id="target-149"></a>
## 149. Question replacement

seeing that a different determination is needed.

**Candidate contributors:** Showing that possible answers would leave the actual decision unchanged; identifying a prerequisite determination; exposing a false presupposition.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Changing the question must preserve or explicitly revise the underlying purpose.

<a id="target-150"></a>
## 150. Question priority

changing which uncertainty is worth addressing first.

**Candidate contributors:** The consequence of resolving each uncertainty; dependency order; available discriminating information; cost of delay or inquiry.

**Conditions and distinctions:** The easiest or most interesting question may have little effect on the current activity.

<a id="target-151"></a>
## 151. Answerability

recognizing what could or could not settle a question.

**Candidate contributors:** A defined result type; an available observation or derivation that separates alternatives; recognition of inaccessible data or incompatible premises.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Lack of a current answer is different from impossibility in principle.

<a id="target-152"></a>
## 152. Evidence requirement

changing what information would bear on the inquiry.

**Candidate contributors:** Competing accounts that predict different observations; identifying relevant units and scope; distinguishing testimony, measurement, and derivation.

**Conditions and distinctions:** More information is useful only if it bears on the unresolved alternatives.

<a id="target-153"></a>
## 153. Investigation direction

changing where an inquiry should proceed.

**Candidate contributors:** A newly exposed dependency; a source with the missing information; a derivable consequence; an informative failure of the current route.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Continue from the unresolved relation, not from the mere availability of another source.

<a id="target-154"></a>
## 154. Completion judgment

changing whether the inquiry is sufficiently resolved.

**Candidate contributors:** A result satisfying the stated use; elimination or containment of consequential uncertainty; recognition of changed completion criteria.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Artifact completion is not inquiry completion; residual uncertainty may be acceptable for one use and not another.
