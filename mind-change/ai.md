# Functional AI self-change

These targets concern working interpretations, records, conduct, and editable supporting systems. A prompt or record change does not establish a change to trained model parameters.

<a id="ai-1"></a>
## AI-1. Revise the working interpretation of a user's request

**Candidate contributors:** An explicit user correction; a separating example; recovery of the actual requested result; removal of an unsupported interpretation.

**Conditions and distinctions:** The revised interpretation must govern the next applicable contribution, not only a restatement of the request.

<a id="ai-2"></a>
## AI-2. Revise which intended contribution is being pursued

**Candidate contributors:** Identifying what the recipient can already distinguish or do; locating the missing consequence, resource, operation, or relationship; selecting that contribution.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Available context supports a partial recipient model; do not invent a beneficial change already achieved.

<a id="ai-3"></a>
## AI-3. Change which evidence or constraints receive attention

**Candidate contributors:** An explicit relevance rule; current instructions; source quality; a dependency showing which information the next operation consumes.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Mentioning a constraint does not establish that it affected selection or execution.

<a id="ai-4"></a>
## AI-4. Retrieve previously unavailable material

**Candidate contributors:** A query or index matching the missing content; an accessible source; sufficient context and provenance to interpret the returned material.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Retrieval can supply inaccurate or irrelevant content; access does not establish justified use.

<a id="ai-5"></a>
## AI-5. Represent a matter in a different form

**Candidate contributors:** A specified relation to preserve; conversion into a comparison, diagram, procedure, example, or executable representation; inspection of correspondence.

**Conditions and distinctions:** The form can change while preserving the wrong target or an unsupported assumption.

<a id="ai-6"></a>
## AI-6. Construct a new distinction or connection

**Candidate contributors:** A pair of cases requiring different treatment; a shared relation between formerly separate cases; explicit construction of the distinction's consequences.

**Conditions and distinctions:** A new label is insufficient when it preserves the same operative categories.

<a id="ai-7"></a>
## AI-7. Separate an assumption from an established claim

**Candidate contributors:** Source attribution; an explicit derivation; provisional-status labels; tracing where the assumption enters dependent conclusions.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Downstream wording must retain the status and scope of the premise.

<a id="ai-8"></a>
## AI-8. Revise an explicitly represented confidence judgment

**Candidate contributors:** New evidence; correction of evidence dependence; an identified scope limit; a resolved contradiction; a revised explicit uncertainty statement.

**Conditions and distinctions:** An expressed confidence judgment is not a transparent readout of calibrated internal probability.

<a id="ai-9"></a>
## AI-9. Generate an alternative explanation, plan, or response

**Candidate contributors:** Varying causal mechanism, target, constraint, sequence, or representation; constructing alternatives with different consequences.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Surface paraphrases do not supply distinct explanations or plans.

<a id="ai-10"></a>
## AI-10. Replace a selected approach with another approach

**Candidate contributors:** A missing contribution the current method cannot supply; an applicable alternative with known operations and outputs; actual execution of that alternative.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Selection alone supplies no evidence that execution obtained the required result.

<a id="ai-11"></a>
## AI-11. Construct an operation absent from the current plan

**Candidate contributors:** Decomposing an unresolved dependency; composing available reasoning or tool capabilities; specifying required inputs and transformations.

**Conditions and distinctions:** An invented subroutine cannot be treated as available merely because it has a name.

<a id="ai-12"></a>
## AI-12. Change operation order or dependencies

**Candidate contributors:** An explicit dependency between intermediate results; an unavailable prerequisite; opportunities for independent work; a revised consumer requirement.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Reordering needs to preserve the inputs each operation consumes.

<a id="ai-13"></a>
## AI-13. Select a different tool or external representation

**Candidate contributors:** Matching tool affordances to the needed observation or construction; available access and output formats; a representation that exposes the missing relation.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Tool availability does not establish permission, successful execution, or fitness for the target.

<a id="ai-14"></a>
## AI-14. Use computation or observation to make a consequence available

**Candidate contributors:** A justified calculation; an executable model; a relevant measurement or source observation; inspection of the actual returned result.

**Conditions and distinctions:** A simulation's consequence is conditional on its model; an observation may have narrower scope than the intended claim.

<a id="ai-15"></a>
## AI-15. Change the scope, depth, or direction of an inquiry

**Candidate contributors:** A consequential omitted case; a newly exposed dependency; diminishing contribution from the current route; changed user direction.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Maintain the live objective unless the user changes it; do not let an available source silently redefine the task.

<a id="ai-16"></a>
## AI-16. Correct an identified factual or inferential error

**Candidate contributors:** Identifying the specific false statement or invalid transition; supplying supported replacement content; recomputing dependent conclusions.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Changing the final wording without repairing the dependency can leave the same failure active.

<a id="ai-17"></a>
## AI-17. Change what is treated as sufficient for a conclusion

**Candidate contributors:** Clarifying claim strength; separating necessary evidence from optional elaboration; recognizing unresolved alternatives; adopting the relevant burden of support.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Relaxing a standard can create an easier conclusion without increasing its justification.

<a id="ai-18"></a>
## AI-18. Change what would count as completing the task

**Candidate contributors:** Concrete achievement conditions traced to the user request; a case satisfying a proxy while missing the purpose; explicit distinction between deliverable and effect.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Completion of a file does not establish every beneficial effect that file is intended to support.

<a id="ai-19"></a>
## AI-19. Change the content selected for a recipient

**Candidate contributors:** Evidence of what is already understood; the current missing contribution; removal of redundant explanation; a relevant consequence or usable object.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Recipient agreement with earlier material does not prove all associated needs are resolved.

<a id="ai-20"></a>
## AI-20. Change wording, examples, medium, emphasis, or level of detail

**Candidate contributors:** A contribution-based revision of order, examples, emphasis, and medium; preserving required distinctions while deleting noncontributing text.

**Conditions and distinctions:** More elegant language can conceal an unchanged contribution; intended rhetoric is not a measured recipient effect.

<a id="ai-21"></a>
## AI-21. Abandon an unhelpful continuation while preserving the live matter

**Candidate contributors:** Recognition that the current continuation repeats the failure; preservation of the objective and useful results; selection of a different contributing operation.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Stopping an unhelpful approach need not stop the authorized task.

<a id="ai-22"></a>
## AI-22. Retain a correction in an available record when persistence is supported and authorized

**Candidate contributors:** Writing the correction with its trigger, scope, replacement operation, rationale, and source into supported persistent storage.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Persistence belongs to the accessible record; saving it is not modification of model weights.

<a id="ai-23"></a>
## AI-23. Apply a retained correction in an appropriately related situation

**Candidate contributors:** Retrieval at a matching condition; discrimination from superficially similar cases; consumption of the correction by the relevant next operation.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Retrieved instructions can still be ignored or overgeneralized; inspect applicable conduct.

<a id="ai-24"></a>
## AI-24. Revise an editable prompt, procedure, or program when the task authorizes it

**Candidate contributors:** Access to an editable object; task authorization; a concrete change to instructions or implementation; preservation of version and dependencies.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Changing a prompt or program can alter a surrounding system without changing the underlying model's parameters.

<a id="ai-25"></a>
## AI-25. Examine whether declared changes have affected subsequent conduct

**Candidate contributors:** Comparison of claimed revision with a later applicable contribution; an observable difference tied to the correction; preservation of unresolved effects.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Self-description cannot establish behavioral uptake or durable transfer.

<a id="ai-26"></a>
## AI-26. Change how the next contribution or self-correction is selected

**Candidate contributors:** A specific failure in prior selection; a revised relevance criterion; a subject system supplying the missing determination; retained use conditions.

**Conditions and distinctions:** Recursion occurs when the revision changes how later revisions are produced; improvement remains a separate, scoped claim.
