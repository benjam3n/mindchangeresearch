# PERSUA 8x — permission, not persuasion

Intended mind change: Stop assuming unchanged action means a belief-directed message failed.

Actual starting judgment: If Alex does not publish after receiving evidence, another, stronger evidence message looks like the next attempt.

Concrete input: `frozen-inputs.md`, Alex’s four explicit statements, 17:00 deadline, and later doubt boundary.

## Target

TARGET: Alex, report publisher without current publish permission. GOAL: corrected report is published by 17:00. CONTEXT: direct work message. RELATIONSHIP: peer; no authority to grant permission.

## Belief map — 12 beliefs

- B1 Alex accepts the corrected calculation.
- B2 The correction should replace the old result.
- B3 Publication requires role permission.
- B4 Alex’s current role lacks that permission.
- B5 Permission can be granted by an authorized administrator.
- B6 The deadline is 17:00.
- B7 Publishing without permission violates the control policy.
- B8 Compliance matters more than appearing fast.
- B9 Another explanation of the arithmetic will not change B4.
- B10 A prepared report is not a published report.
- B11 Alex will publish after permission is granted.
- B12 The current blocker is practical access, not stated evidential disagreement.

Values: correctness, compliance, traceability, meeting the deadline, and not misrepresenting completion. Identity connection: Alex’s role identity supports compliance; bypass language would threaten it. Known: B1–B4, B6, B11 are stated. Unknown: administrator availability and grant latency. No emotion is stated or inferred.

## Seven resistance points

- R1 PRACTICAL/HIGH: no publish permission. Addressable only by an authorized grant or authorized publisher.
- R2 SOCIAL/HIGH: peer messenger lacks authority to change access. Addressable by routing.
- R3 PRACTICAL/MEDIUM: grant latency may exceed 17:00. Partly addressable by escalation.
- R4 IDENTITY/MEDIUM: asking Alex to bypass controls conflicts with compliant-role identity. Do not work around.
- R5 EVIDENTIAL/LOW: calculation is already accepted; further proof is nondiagnostic.
- R6 MOTIVATIONAL/LOW: willingness is explicitly conditional on permission; no contrary motivation is observed.
- R7 PRACTICAL/MEDIUM: “publication” may require another workflow step after permission. Addressable by confirming the action interface.

PRIMARY RESISTANCE: R1. If R1 is unchanged, message quality cannot itself create the authorized state transition.

## Four paths

1. B1+B11 → permission is the missing condition → authorized administrator grants access → Alex publishes.
2. B2+B7 → control-compliant publication matters → authorized publisher publishes on Alex’s prepared artifact.
3. B6+B10 → preparation is insufficient before deadline → route exact artifact and deadline to owner → owner executes.
4. Boundary path after permission: doubt about independence → show A1/A2 lineage → calculate one or two admissible likelihood factors → Alex decides on correction.

Paths 1–3 are coordination paths, not belief persuasion. Path 4 is evidential persuasion only after the practical gate moves.

## Twelve message elements

1. Opening: “The corrected report is ready; your publish permission is still missing.”
2. State accepted common ground: Alex accepts the correction.
3. Name exact blocked action: publish.
4. Name exact missing condition: publish role.
5. Preserve compliance: do not ask for bypass.
6. Ask administrator for grant by a specified cutoff.
7. Provide fallback: authorized owner publishes the prepared artifact.
8. Attach artifact identifier, not another arithmetic explanation.
9. State deadline 17:00.
10. Require a publication receipt before saying “published.”
11. If grant is denied, record denial rather than blame Alex.
12. If Alex later disputes evidence, switch to lineage and likelihood rather than repeat access requests.

What not to say: “You still don’t understand,” “just publish it,” urgency/social-pressure language, or a longer calculation. These contradict B1, B7, or R1.

Voice: short technical-peer sentences; Alex’s terms “publish permission,” “corrected calculation,” and “17:00.” No internal terms such as locus, state transition, or persuasion architecture.

## Ethics

Transparency: clean; the full strategy says exactly that access, not resistance, is the diagnosed gate. Autonomy: clean; Alex may decline or report another blocker. Reciprocity: clean. Manipulation flags rejected: shame, manufactured urgency, identity attack, and hiding the fallback publisher.

Key moves: route to authority; preserve compliance; ask for one executable access action; use an authorized-publisher fallback.
