## Strongest correction

Messages are not the general unit of mind change. A message is selected only when evidence indicates the controlling transition is message-addressable. The more general unit is a typed, admissible state transition at a locus, with an operator-specific receipt. Physical action, permission, representation, memory access, capability, attention, value constraint, and belief update therefore enter the same compiler without being reduced to persuasion.

Actual mind change: I changed from “choose the best procedure or message” to “compile the smallest supported and authorized transition, then select a mechanism capable of moving its actual locus.” I also stopped treating raw KEEP frequency as benefit evidence.

Benefit or harm: The cycle changed operations on recipient, belief, memory, value, physical-action, evaluation, experiment, and capability cases while retaining null and unresolved outcomes. No human mind or physical location is claimed to have changed.
