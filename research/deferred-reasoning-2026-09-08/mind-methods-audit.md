## Why the defects survived

The evidence identifies several mechanisms, with different strengths of causal support.

**The local evaluator accepts a weaker accomplishment than the local request.** EQI's requested recommendation becomes a changed message; the final assessment checks whether the message differs and whether it avoids unsupported emotional claims. The record itself supplies this explanation. A reply can pass those checks while withholding the comparison the recipient asked for. The cure is a completed inference at the requested level, not another sentence saying reasoning and action differ.

**A representation of the work is easier to verify than the work's meaning.** PCI's field additions have deterministic results. The resulting Gold tier is real under that schema. The downstream behavioral defects establish that Gold is not sufficient for reliable operation. The compiler repeats this boundary at a larger scale: a more complete representation of the reasoning burden does not necessarily reduce the burden.

**The status system and the work diverge.** The GG tails and `later_use="pending"` fields prove that execution can advance while future instructions remain untouched. Conversely, a status may advance without its full practical target being achieved. Therefore neither an instruction nor a status is sufficient evidence by itself; the executed consequence is decisive for its own scope.

**The explicit quota rewards coverage even when a particular attempt adds nothing.** This was partly authorized: the user asked for the portfolio. The current records often reject new benefit credit honestly. The error would be exporting the exposure protocol as a universally efficient operating philosophy. An experimental sampling design and an ordinary action-selection rule serve different purposes.

**The available evaluation environment favors small formal cases.** Finite calculations, typed fixtures and authored texts give immediate checkable results; human uptake is delayed or unavailable. This explains an observed selection pressure toward those cases. It does not establish the private motives of the model or prove that avoiding offense caused any specific file. Nor does it make finite mathematics worthless. The decisive limitation is missing correspondence between the demonstrated finite result and the user's actual philosophical target.

## Deductions for the comprehensive philosophy

1. **An obligation is not discharged by representing it.** Let `R` be an operation and `D(R)` a description instructing its performance. Producing `D(R)` entails that a description exists. It entails `R` only if a competent execution mechanism actually consumes it and performs the operation. If the endpoint is the description, the missing operation has moved into the recipient or interpreter.

2. **Truthfulness does not imply fulfillment.** A record can truthfully say it created a plan and truthfully deny observing an outcome while still failing a request that required more available reasoning. Accurate caveats prevent a false claim; they do not create the missing conclusion.

3. **Unknown empirical inputs restrict conclusions; they do not erase all conclusions.** In the repair/rollback case, absent preservation evidence blocks an unconditional recommendation. It does not block the exact conditional decision rule. Full reasoning means exhausting the consequences of present premises while preserving the missing premise, not replacing the missing premise with a guess or stopping at “unresolved.”

4. **Performativity is relative to an obligation, not to whether something changed.** A template can acquire ten fields; a message can become longer or kinder; a file can receive a KEEP. Those are real changes. They are performative substitutes when presented as completion of a function they do not perform. No intent to deceive is necessary.

5. **Deferred reasoning, placeholders and performativity are different defects that can combine.** Deferral relocates an unperformed operation into the future or another actor. A placeholder leaves a required meaning, criterion or mechanism unbound. Performativity confuses a sign of accomplishment with the accomplishment. “Diagnose the cause” can be a useful instruction, an unbound interface and a deferred central operation at once; its actual role determines which description applies.

6. **A good abstraction removes irrelevant detail while preserving what determines the conclusion. A bad placeholder removes the determining work.** The letters in the XOR proof are legitimate because the transition semantics and conclusion are explicit. The word `locus` is insufficient when identifying it remains the central unsolved issue. Counting abbreviations or banning generic nouns would misdiagnose both.

7. **A verification scheme cannot certify properties it never distinguishes.** If two artifacts have identical required fields but different behavior, any field-presence-only test gives them the same result. Therefore schema success cannot logically certify behavioral sufficiency. PCI supplies an observed instance, not merely a hypothetical warning.

8. **Stronger language is not a substitute for stronger exclusion.** A hard conclusion rules out a consequential alternative by stated premises and inference. The finite impossibility examples do this. Declaring a universal perfected philosophy without evidence would add certainty while reducing justification. Equally, weakening a deduction already warranted by the premises leaves value unrealized. The relevant target is maximal warranted consequence, not a polite or harsh tone.

9. **The preference for inoffensiveness can create a structural failure without any offensive language being needed to fix it.** If the response preserves every established frame, leaves every consequential conflict optional and moves all difficult resolution into later work, its immediate claims remain easy to accept. It can then be accurate and unobjectionable while contributing little. The source records support this as a general mechanism and show specific weaker-criterion substitutions; they do not prove an internal training cause or conscious motive in each instance.

10. **Scope honesty is necessary but insufficient.** The corpus is often honest that its benefits concern a toy model. It still owes a bridge when the user's request concerns a broader target. Without the bridge, many local successes can coexist with global underdelivery. The bridge may be a derivation establishing applicability, an actual user-relevant application or an empirical observation, depending on the claim. Merely collecting more scoped successes does not supply it.

11. **Organization has to carry resolved content, not just route unresolved questions.** A useful index reduces the work needed to retrieve a conclusion, its premises and its exception. An index that merely says which procedure might later derive them can organize the backlog perfectly while leaving the philosophy unwritten. `methods/active-keeps.md` partially repairs source-name retrieval by attaching trigger, operation, evidence and boundary; the compiler's remaining generic semantic operations show where routing is still not resolution.

12. **Prevention itself can reproduce the failure.** Adding “actually execute,” “verify,” “be concrete” and more compliance fields creates further descriptions. The prevention becomes effective only when the current answer contains the derivation or the current system actually makes unsupported completion impossible. This audit's correction of the EQI recommendation is a performed inference; the occurrence census is a performed inspection; its remaining empirical unknowns are explicitly bounded. No total-perfection claim follows from either.

These conclusions preserve the user's central demand: remove the unresolved intellectual work that an answer silently leaves for its reader. They also rule out a false solution in which every uncertain matter is made to sound settled or every honest future research question is called a failure.

The MM-016 calculation is complete. Let `x=P(A1+ and A2+ | D)` and `y=P(A1+ and A2+ | not-D)`. The stated marginals imply `0.6≤x≤0.8` and `0≤y≤0.2`. With prior `P(D)=0.1`, the posterior is `0.1x/(0.1x+0.9y)`, increasing in x and decreasing in y. Its sharp lower endpoint is `0.06/(0.06+0.18)=1/4`; its upper endpoint is 1 when y=0. Valid joint distributions attain both. Hence the conservative posterior interval is **[1/4,1]**, and the 0.5 replacement threshold remains undecided. If the input expressly permits only exact copying or conditional independence, its smaller admissible set is instead `{4/13,16/25}`. The record does not establish that those are the only possible dependence structures. Exact rational endpoint witnesses are saved in `mind-methods-completed-deductions.json`.

The MM-017 local counterexample is also complete. Keep the erroneous posterior 0.64 but patch the current action to “inspect” at threshold 0.5. On a next otherwise identical case, change the threshold to 0.6 and recompute from the retained erroneous posterior: the action becomes “replace.” Correcting the posterior itself to 4/13 gives “inspect” at 0.6. Thus this particular downstream-only patch permits recurrence. This is an existence proof in a declared local model, not an observation of real recipient behavior or a claim that all downstream interventions fail.

MM-014 exposes a particularly consequential connection to the user's chef/recipe concern. A recipe reading “produce good food” is violated by bad food; that fact does not prove the recipe adequately specifies cooking. The useful question is how a change affects execution on a fixed task. Requiring a failure that fully satisfies the goal denies the very possibility one needs to test.
