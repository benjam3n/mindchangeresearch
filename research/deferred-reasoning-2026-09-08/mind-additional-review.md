## MRA-L01: operation choices are called dialogue variants

`methods/utp-01.md:7` advertises “three concrete dialogue variants.” Line 29 defines the nearest milestone as selecting an appropriate move and then performing it. Line 31 says the three variants test that milestone, but its actual content is:

> For “Is every surprising claim worth investigating?”, the next move is a counterexample and an inquiry-selection distinction.

The other two entries similarly select a kind of variation or a kind of answer. They do not supply the actual counterexample, changed image, or answer. This does **not** establish three performed dialogue responses. It establishes three mappings from a stated activity to a proposed operation.

The record itself later uses the more accurate label “selection examples” at line 43. Line 33 also supplies a separate actual creative output and withholds an unsupported interpretation in a second case. Lines 37–41 explicitly deny a distinct new operational improvement and record `REJECT` as a new beneficial change. The preserved original `sources/root-utp.md` asks for constructing an ideal, stress-testing it, and deriving priorities and a milestone; it does not require deploying an ideal conversation. Those core obligations are addressed.

The precise classification is therefore **overstated demonstration scope**, not an unperformed UTP application, counterfeit human effect, or false KEEP. The source's legitimate completion cannot be promoted into evidence that every proposed dialogue move was performed.

The missing first example can be completed from its stated question: a surprising claim can be irrelevant to the inquiry, and surprise measures a mismatch with expectation rather than the value of resolving it. For example, an unexpected but unsupported claim about the number of pebbles in an unrelated jar supplies no reason to interrupt a time-limited comparison of two known methods. Investigation requires a connection to something worth determining; surprise alone does not provide it. The second and third example prompts omit the actual image and substantive question, so an exact image transformation or factual answer cannot be derived from those prompts alone. The record legitimately demonstrates operation selection there, and no more.

Calling them all performative because they use a reflection template would confuse a presentation convention with absence of work.

The relation between the broader research goal and these local achievements remains a separate question. Finite arithmetic and constructive examples do not establish a universally reliable perspective-improvement system. Their limited scope also does not erase what they demonstrate. The correct account retains both propositions: these records contain completed intellectual work; that work cannot automatically lend its authority to the larger program's reliability claims.

## Review of the root synthesis

The full synthesis `When-descriptions-replace-reasoning.md` was reviewed. Its principal distinctions and logically scoped counterexample arguments are sound as written. Two narrow claims require correction:

1. Repository presence of a correction demonstrates that **storing or repeating** it is insufficient to prevent accepted defects. It does not establish that the model generating each defect actually remembered or understood that correction. “Merely remembering your correction cannot be sufficient: the correction is already present” overstates what repository evidence shows. The supported replacement is: “Merely storing or repeating the correction is insufficient; it is already written into the projects.”
2. The proposed conditional repair/rollback recommendation gives repair priority when both can preserve reviewed changes. Preservation alone does not imply that priority. The supported present decision is to preserve a snapshot; qualify both options against correction and preservation; select the only qualifying option if exactly one qualifies; compare the stated criteria if both qualify; and withhold a choice if neither has the required evidence. No unconditional repair-before-rollback ordering follows without additional costs, effects, or preferences.

Both concerns were sent to the root agent during the review. No other strongest philosophical claim required weakening merely to make the account less consequential.
