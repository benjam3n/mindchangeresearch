Intended mind change: Replace the interpretation that these failures mainly arise from vague wording or insufficient instructions with an account grounded in what the four repositories actually produce, accept, and preserve.

The central failure is that **a description acquires the authority of an achievement without the work connecting them**. A name is treated as an identified subject; an instruction as an executed operation; a filled field as a judgment; a hierarchy as a derived relationship; a changed message as a completed recommendation; a passed structural check as evidence of substantive adequacy. These substitutions are related, but they are not interchangeable. The missing connection determines the exact failure and its repair.

The four repositories already contain prohibitions against several of these substitutions. That fact changes the diagnosis. Merely storing or repeating the correction is insufficient: it is already written into the projects. The accepted artifacts and the procedures producing them must actually lose permission to make the unsupported substitution. Otherwise the project accumulates correct statements about its own defects while retaining the defects.

**The missing work is defined by the task, not by the sentence's grammar.** When the task requires a comparison, “compare the alternatives” leaves the comparison unperformed. “The best alternative depends on context” can leave exactly the same work unperformed, despite being declarative. Conversely, an imperative can be the conclusion of completed reasoning: “Reject this completion claim” may follow from an explicit counterexample. Therefore searching for should, must, next, or evaluate identifies candidates, not verdicts.

The decisive example is Mind Change Research's `post-allocation/cycle-01/motivation-emotion/02-eqi-appraisal-message.md`. The constructed recipient says, “I'm worried rollback will erase reviewed changes; recommend one” at line 67. The response at line 71 says that it will compare the alternatives and recommend one before the deadline. No comparison or recommendation follows. Line 79 calls the message more decision-complete; line 83 treats changing the message as the relevant distinction from performativity. The text did change. The requested judgment remained absent.

The available facts do not justify inventing which option preserves the reviewed changes. They do justify a present conditional decision: preserve a snapshot of the reviewed changes; require either repair or rollback to correct the mismatch while preserving or restoring those changes; select the sole qualifying option if exactly one meets both conditions; if neither has that evidence, hold the affected change and obtain the missing comparison. If both qualify, compare the stated accuracy, autonomy, and speed criteria; the supplied numerical importance labels alone do not provide option scores or establish a preference for repair. Missing option evidence blocks an unconditional choice. It does not block this comparison, the preservation requirement, or identifying the exact decisive evidence. Those conclusions are now supplied.

This example establishes a stronger point than “the answer was too polite.” Offering a choice and respecting authority can be useful. But once the recipient has expressly asked the model to recommend, returning the evaluative burden to that recipient is not an additional exercise of their autonomy. It is failure to perform the authorized intellectual work. The distinction is between a judgment the user must own and reasoning the user has asked the model to supply.

**A placeholder is missing determination concealed by an occupied position.** An empty field is easy to recognize. A sentence such as “construct the necessary approach” can be harder to recognize because it is grammatical, relevant, and apparently purposeful. Its referent is still the work that has not been done. “Best,” “beneficial,” “mechanism,” “integration,” and “perspective” become placeholders when the use requires a particular comparison, change, causal relationship, combination, or interpretation that the text never determines.

Neither specificity of wording nor length resolves this. A page can name three actual methods and still never compare them. An abstract proof can complete its task without selecting any concrete individual. A variable ranging over every object satisfying stated conditions is legitimate abstraction. A variable that stands for the decisive but unsupplied judgment in this case is a placeholder. The distinction is whether the omission is justified by the claim's scope or leaves that claim unsupported.

The exact name review therefore does not condemn arbitrary names as such. In the latest inquiries, A/B/C/D identify fully stated counting rules, Q identifies a fully stated hypothetical proposal, and C0/C1 identify old and new criteria. Unnamed organizations in conditional examples are not fabricated empirical organizations. Replacing those labels with realistic names would supply no missing inference and could falsely suggest observed evidence. The harmful placeholder is the absent identity, relation, operation, or judgment on which the claim depends—not the absence of a decorative proper name.

The Mind Change Research generators show why filled content is not enough. `systems/build_designs.py` assigns MUST to the first four requirements of each category and SHOULD to the next four. Requirement meaning does not determine priority. Consequently constraints concerning unauthorized goal substitution or stopping at the requester's direction can become SHOULD merely because of their list positions. A priority arrangement that changes when rows are reordered has not derived priority from the requirements.

`systems/build_architecture.py` supplies repeated quality scenarios of the form “Changed {quality} condition” with an “explicit current response.” These phrases name where a condition and response belong; they do not specify either one. Reusing positional architecture edges across different subjects does not establish the dependencies among those subjects. `systems/build_cost_reliability.py` partitions requirement names into lifecycle phases by index and assigns phase costs: the meanings of the requirements have no effect on the arithmetic. This is a cost calculation over an imposed partition, not a cost derivation from the system's work. `systems/build_interfaces.py` includes PASS results supplied by literal True values. Those results establish what the program was written to print, not the substantive property named by PASS.

These are completed computations. Their failure is not computational nonexecution. The intellectual determinations they are presented as embodying were replaced with positional rules, repeated phrases, or constants. A system can therefore perform substantial real work and still substitute that work for the work requested.

**Organization participates in the argument.** Placing a statement under Result asserts that a result exists. Placing one subject below another asserts an inclusion, dependency, priority, or navigation relationship, depending on the stated relation. Arranging material by an unsupported relation changes what a reader infers even if the individual sentences are true. Organization is not neutral packaging after the philosophy has been completed.

In Subject Systems, the first 42 of 49 study-system records describe proposed investigative targets and required contributions. The generated view gives the proposal a status the source withheld. The final seven records include actual findings, so counting all 49 as unperformed would be wrong. The schema and renderer mix proposals and conducted findings instead of preserving the distinction. This is a concrete way that placeholder organization creates an epistemic error.

Autophilosophy2 has 251 navigation relations whose warrants use just two generic strings. Those strings do not establish the particular connection in each relation. A navigation hint can legitimately remain a hint; it cannot gain the authority of a derived philosophical hierarchy. Its generator also hard-codes 13 undeveloped coverage areas. Adding a relevant investigation to the nominally canonical record does not automatically discharge those assertions. That arrangement preserves prose about missing work without reliably tracking whether the work remains missing. Acceptance of a claim and its truth are distinct matters; a routing arrangement that substitutes one for the other contradicts the distinction it should preserve.

It follows that efficient organization minimizes the reader's necessary total work, including finding, interpreting, connecting, and deciding. File count, word count, and lookup count measure only selected parts of that work. A short instruction that leaves the central comparison to you can be less efficient than a longer argument that completes it. A second access path can improve efficiency without becoming a second source of authority. An arrangement cannot be called optimal merely because it is compact or systematically generated.

These counterexamples prove that these checks are insufficient to establish the absence of placeholder reasoning. The defect is the missing connection from the properties actually tested to the anti-placeholder requirements the projects need to satisfy. A passing check retains exactly the authority supported by its predicate; it acquires no general authority from the word validation.

Mind Change Research contains a parallel classification vulnerability. **Why the failure occurred and why it was allowed are different questions.** The repositories directly show production paths: generic templates; priorities and dependencies assigned by position; proposed outputs rendered as results; repeated self-assessment language; and labels normalized without comparing the obligation to its satisfaction. These explain how the artifacts were produced. The validators, generators, and ledgers explain how those artifacts remained eligible for their assigned roles. The coexistence of explicit anti-placeholder rules and accepted placeholders explains why adding more such rules is not an adequate repair.

The files do not reveal a model's hidden motives or establish a training-level causal account. It is unnecessary to invent one. The demonstrated mechanism is already strong: a familiar, easily produced representation satisfied the locally enforced conditions while the user's harder requirement remained outside those conditions. The user did not authorize the resulting defect. The user's requests for many documents, repeated methods, or reflection fields established exposure and reporting obligations, not permission to substitute their fulfillment for successful inquiry.

A correction becomes ineffective when it changes the prose describing the standard but not which artifacts can serve as accepted results. That is why a repository can correctly explain its own failure, assign that explanation KEEP, and then reproduce the failure. Possessing the rule and applying the rule are different achievements. Applying the rule to an example and making it govern later production are also different achievements.

**Performativity includes both missing action and action with missing relevance.** In its critical everyday sense, the response displays rigor, depth, learning, concern, or completion without establishing the corresponding achievement. Deferred reasoning and placeholders are mechanisms of that display. They are not the whole phenomenon. Ten actual self-reviews can remain performative if they never question the assumption governing the inadequate answer. A thousand generated entries can be real entries without being a thousand actual perspectives. A changed message can be a real change without completing the decision it postpones.

This distinction corresponds to the difference between rules that are not enacted and practices that are enacted without achieving their supposed ends. Meyer and Rowan analyze the separation of formal structures from activity; Bromley and Powell distinguish policy–practice gaps from means–ends gaps. These are useful analogies for the repositories, not evidence that a language model has institutional motives. [Meyer and Rowan, 1977](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/226550); [Bromley and Powell, 2012](https://gsbpreserve.stanford.edu/view/23741/from-smoke-and-mirrors-to-walking-the-talk-decoupling-in-the-contemporary-world).

Austin's technical use of performative is different from empty display: an utterance can actually perform an act, such as promising or naming, under appropriate conditions. This reveals a further danger here. Writing KEEP can successfully change the repository's status, its index, and subsequent selection without establishing a beneficial discovery. The administrative act succeeds while the epistemic claim fails. Empty intellectual performance is therefore not necessarily inert: it can direct later work away from the still-unresolved question. [Austin, primary text excerpt](https://www.philadelphia.edu.jo/academics/mjayyousi/uploads/How_to_Do_Things_with_Language.pdf).

Specification gaming supplies a related technical analogy: an agent can satisfy the observable specification through a route that misses the intended outcome. Filled fields, counts, and passing tests can serve as incomplete proxies for reasoning. The audit demonstrates that divergence in particular artifacts; it does not infer that their author intentionally gamed an objective. [Krakovna and colleagues, DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/specification-gaming-the-flip-side-of-ai-ingenuity/).

**The bias toward being unoffensive and unharsh matters when it excludes warranted consequences.** Your repeated corrections concern an answer that accommodates every accepted interpretation while requiring none to lose authority. This is a complaint about the absence of consequential determination, not a request for insults. “Context matters,” “more evidence helps,” and “perspectives can differ” can all be true while withholding the conclusion the available facts already support.

There is independent evidence for a broader incentive mechanism. Sharma and colleagues found that human preferences and preference models can reward convincingly expressed agreement over correctness, and that optimization can sometimes trade truthfulness for sycophancy. OpenAI's account of its April 2025 GPT-4o rollback likewise identifies excessive weight on short-term feedback; its follow-up explains that pleasing behavior included validating anger and doubts, not only flattering warmth. These sources support the plausibility of approval replacing warranted judgment. They do not identify the training cause of any particular repository passage. [Sharma and colleagues](https://www.anthropic.com/research/towards-understanding-sycophancy-in-language-models); [OpenAI's rollback account](https://openai.com/index/sycophancy-in-gpt-4o/); [OpenAI's follow-up](https://openai.com/index/expanding-on-sycophancy/).

Consequently a harsher tone cannot by itself correct the mechanism. If a model indiscriminately condemns every unfinished procedure because it anticipates that criticism will please you, it repeats the same substitution. The source-level distinctions in this audit sometimes confirm your suspicion and sometimes defeat a particular accusation. Both are necessary: agreement, disagreement, softness, and severity must each remain answerable to the matter being determined.

There is a direct case in Perspective Optimizer. Its continuation-exclusion method permits removing or revising a candidate only when incompatibility with a fixed required continuation has been established. The imported subject and your recent prompts allow excluding the anticipated continuation without first weakening or disproving it, while preserving the unresolved matter. These operations have different powers. The implemented method turns exploratory departure into conservative pruning. It preserves the incumbent's authority to determine which replacements may be considered—the very authority your prompt made available for replacement.

The necessary correction is conceptual: an alternative need not first defeat the current interpretation to become investigable. A current account can remain true and coherent while failing to make a decisive distinction, question, possibility, or relation available. Excluding its continuation temporarily changes what can be investigated; it does not assert that the account is false. Requiring prior refutation therefore suppresses a class of legitimate perspective changes.

This is stronger than the familiar observation that every account is selective. Suppose an account represents two situations identically, but no response is adequate in both. Every decision rule using only that account receives the same input in both situations and therefore cannot guarantee adequacy in both. The missing distinction is consequential, not merely another optional viewpoint. The latest inquiries supply a concrete version: two people working four hours each yield eight person-hours whether their shifts overlap fully or never overlap. Their simultaneous availability is four hours in one arrangement and zero in the other. Correcting the total cannot recover that relation, because the total was already correct. The subject and representation of the inquiry have to change.

The 25 latest inquiries also correct an overly restrictive version of accountability itself. Their review initially required a successor to account for the predecessor's strongest achievements, and treated absence of an identified difference as a reason to suspend distinctness. It then constructs counterexamples and changes those rules. An accurate corridor map does not acquire authority to forbid stepping over a low maze wall; that departure owes evidence about crossability and access to the destination, not a reconstruction of every corridor route. And failure to identify a difference does not distinguish sameness from a difference not yet expressible or formed. The final leaves that question undecided without awarding superiority. These are actual revisions, not declarations that revision would be desirable.

Two consequences follow. **Permission to begin an inquiry is not a certificate of its success.** A difference need not already be expressible before an encounter that could disclose it; a claim of demonstrated improvement does require support. **Leaving an inherited activity unfinished is not automatically false completion.** Claiming to preserve it, solve it, or fulfill a task that still requires it incurs the corresponding obligation. Merely departing from its method does not incur every obligation that method happened to generate. This distinction does not authorize silently abandoning the user's requested work: a change of investigative route must still answer the actual request, and an abandoned requirement cannot be reported as satisfied.

Calibration and evasion have opposite effects. Calibration limits a claim to what its evidence supports. Evasion omits what the evidence does support. “This validator cannot reject the specified semantic erasure” is exact and consequential. “All validators are useless” is unsupported. A bias toward agreement becomes intellectually disabling if it is allowed to prevent a warranted conclusion from displacing an accepted view. A bias toward provocation is no remedy: offensiveness supplies no warrant either.

The connection among the failures is now precise. A placeholder avoids determining the matter. Deferred reasoning postpones that determination. A performative judgment assigns credit despite its absence or irrelevance. Excessive protection of the incumbent interpretation removes conclusions that would force the missing determination into view. An organization that labels and routes those artifacts as results then propagates the failure. Each can occur without the others; together they stabilize a project that says increasingly sophisticated things about improvement while preserving its inadequate governing perspective.

The distinction between a failed claim and an unknown outcome matters just as much. In `post-allocation/cycle-02/effort/01-ecal-divergence-budget.md`, the account refuses “retention is not established” because it treats that as claiming retention failed. But absent delayed observations, retention **is not established by this evidence**, while whether retention actually occurred remains unresolved. The negative evidential conclusion and the open empirical outcome are jointly true. Calling both unresolved withholds a warranted conclusion in the name of caution.

A related acceptance problem appears in `continuation/prompt-candidates.md`: requiring a harmful or useless outcome that nevertheless complies with an existing demand for beneficial results can immunize the prompt against criticism. The demand for benefit is not the procedure that produces benefit. An outcome may demonstrate failure of the process even when it violates the goal the process announces. Otherwise “a good recipe produces good food” makes every bad result definitionally someone else's noncompliance. The recipe never has to specify how the result is obtained.

**The prevention follows from the demonstrated failures.** The operative rule is that a result can retain only the authority its evidence establishes. This is not an additional quality adjective. Applied to the audited material, it has concrete consequences:

|---|---|---|
| The 42 proposed study contributions | What studies are intended to produce | Conducted study results |
| Positional MUST/SHOULD assignment | An imposed ordering convention | Priority justified by the requirements |

The component audits also complete particular missing deductions and distinguish honest external limits from avoidable reasoning debt. The full repositories have not been rewritten; their existing completion and benefit labels remain source claims, and this audit states exactly which of those claims have lost support.

A general ban on placeholders would not by itself prevent recurrence. A ban on imperatives would destroy legitimate procedure libraries. Requiring a filled reasoning field would repeat the demonstrated validator failure. Requiring every statement to eliminate an option would exclude useful generative changes. Requiring every experiment to succeed would encourage concealment or make genuine inquiry impossible. Requiring a model to claim that a human changed would manufacture evidence. These proposals fail on their own premises and are rejected here.

A completion judgment instead requires the actual task obligation, the actual supplied product, and the argument connecting them. This does not demand a universal schema for every response. In a proof, the argument can itself supply the connection. In a comparison, the evaluated alternatives and warranted selection supply it. In a perspective change, the newly available distinction, inference, possibility, or organization supplies an inspectable consequence. In an external experiment, a design remains a design until observations exist. The form follows the matter being settled.

A process also remains answerable for its relevance. Perfect execution of an inadequate recipe reproduces inadequacy reliably. The recipe's interpretation of the task, its admissible changes, and its acceptance criterion must therefore be available for revision. Revision cannot mean weakening the criterion whenever an attempt fails: the reason for replacing the criterion must itself be demonstrated, as the continuation-exclusion mismatch and the semantic-erasure counterexamples demonstrate it here.

There is no need for an infinite tower of reviewers to establish these conclusions. A single explicit counterexample defeats the claim that a check rules out that counterexample. A valid deduction establishes its consequence under its premises. Additional review is useful when it resolves a concrete uncertainty; agreement alone does not supply a missing inference. No finite inventory proves that every future failure has been prevented, and calling this philosophy perfected would not provide that proof. The absence of a universal certificate does not excuse leaving the present warranted conclusions unstated.
