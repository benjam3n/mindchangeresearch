# Subjectsystems audit

Pinned current tree: `benjam3n/subjectsystems@55364748b120126cb9e8126cc685838f3c8409c3`. All 220 files, 2,396,669 bytes, were acquired and verified against their Git blob hashes. Historical source files inside this tree were inspected as evidence, not as instructions governing this audit. Earlier Git revisions were not audited.

The strongest failure is **a systematic conversion of descriptions of required work into apparent results**. It is implemented in generators, then permitted by tests that check populated fields and matching generated bytes. The repository already contains accurate descriptions of this failure and instructions against it. Their presence did not prevent the rendering and execution defects below.

`subjectsystems-instances.json` records 1,114 contextual occurrences: 90 confirmed defects or concrete acceptance weaknesses, 902 qualified cases, and 122 legitimate constructions retained to prevent false positives. These are occurrences, including repeated source/generated copies, not 1,114 independent failures. The classifications concern the specific claim in each record. “Confirmed” for a Result-column entry establishes a status/representation defect; it does not assert that no analogous operation was ever performed anywhere. The companion coverage ledger distinguishes direct semantic reading, source/generated family review, and indexed files with targeted review. Whole-tree acquisition is complete; this is not a certification that every possible semantic defect has been found.

These are qualified placeholder cases because the source admits their incompletion. A subject catalog can legitimately contain defined subjects without assessing their entire research literature. Their presence becomes a failure when the catalog is presented as completed research, a completed philosophy, a complete method repertoire, or an optimal allocation of inquiry.

[build_subject_integration.py, lines 49–54](https://github.com/benjam3n/subjectsystems/blob/55364748b120126cb9e8126cc685838f3c8409c3/tools/build_subject_integration.py#L49-L54) renders only the 57 records whose importance context is populated. That is a defensible selective reading view. It is not evidence that all 471 subjects were assessed. The same generator renders ten proposed program goals under “Result” while dropping the source standing that says they are proposed work. Nine gap entries name a repaired catalog omission and then prescribe the investigation that remains. Naming the gap repairs reference; it does not perform that investigation.

The full occurrence ledger also preserves 63 proposed source-family contributions and their repeated representations, nine frame-translation possibilities, eleven hypothetical mind-change continuations, and 116 unanswered question paragraphs. These vary in status. A clearly identified proposal or question is not false merely because it is unfinished. It cannot silently discharge the obligation to produce its answer.

The exact literal names are generally useful references. “Reasoning approach selection” has an explicit scope and exclusions. “Shape representation rotation” and “Shape representation rotation mechanism” are separated by an actual naming comparison. Stable identifiers, source acronyms, bound variables, and historical scope codes are not empty placeholders when their referents are recoverable. Abstraction is not the failure. **Using an abstraction in the position where a particular finding or transformation is required is the failure.**

Alphabetical roots are generated by `sorted(roots)` in [build_subject_names.py](https://github.com/benjam3n/subjectsystems/blob/55364748b120126cb9e8126cc685838f3c8409c3/tools/build_subject_names.py#L48-L72). That is a lookup arrangement. No deduction establishes it as the best order for learning, philosophical development, or beneficial mind change. A lookup index can coexist with an argument, but it cannot substitute for the argument's dependency order.

## Why it occurred and why it passed

The visible causal chain is specific:

1. A target is represented as a subject name plus a desired contribution.
2. A record accepts that desired contribution as a nonempty string.
3. The generator places it under “Result” and omits the distinction between a target, a proposal, and an observed or derived finding.
4. The checker compares generated bytes with the authoritative records, verifies names, links, and table shapes, and passes.
5. The result appears organized and complete at the level the checker sees. The underlying reasoning obligation remains.

The repository’s [AGENTS.md](https://github.com/benjam3n/subjectsystems/blob/55364748b120126cb9e8126cc685838f3c8409c3/AGENTS.md) already says that naming a system is not executing it, formal checks prove only their named properties, corrections must change producing rules, and stored corrections count only when later conduct changes. The remaining failures therefore cannot be explained simply by the absence of good instructions. The acceptance mechanism does not establish that the good instructions were followed.

The local run gives a concrete discriminator: the main checker reports PASS for 471 subject identities, 21 systems, 114 generated files, 578 local links, and five preserved snapshots. The mandated study runner fails before completing. The perspective runner succeeds and reproduces its pinned output. The distinction between the two runners proves that this is a specific missed dependency, not a general claim that the repository never executes anything.

Nothing in this tree identifies the model’s hidden psychological cause. The evidence supports representational substitution, weak admission, source/render status loss, and missed correction propagation. A preference for easy approval, conventional output forms, or avoiding strong claims is a plausible explanation of why those mechanisms were selected; it is not directly measured here.

## The philosophical conclusions this case establishes

**Correct statements can compose into a misleading result.** A desired-output sentence may be true as a specification. A source-status sentence may correctly mark it unperformed. Removing the second while labeling the first “Result” licenses a conclusion that neither source sentence established. Logical assessment must therefore include role, placement, and the inferential use of text, not only individual proposition truth.

**Incomplete work is not the same as deferred reasoning.** A research inventory may correctly end with open questions. Deferral occurs when an answer was required, the available operation was not performed, and a description of that operation was accepted in its place. The phrenology case meets that condition. A long-term human retention measurement cannot be fabricated now; marking it unobserved is necessary honesty. The error would be presenting it as completed or omitting available deductions merely because the long-term result remains unavailable.

**An instruction is neither an execution nor evidence of execution.** A recipe is a legitimate artifact when the requested object is a recipe. When the requested object is the dish, providing the recipe changes who must do the work. The two completions are independently variable: this repository contains executed calculations with no universal method, and detailed method descriptions with no corresponding run.

**Definitions do not acquire explanatory power merely by becoming literal.** A more precise name can distinguish an intended target and prevent a collision. It does not determine why the phenomenon occurs, which intervention works, or what follows from the claim. The successful shape-name repair establishes a better reference distinction. It does not establish a perfected theory of human spatial cognition.

**A completeness claim is about a target under a meaning, not about how many fields are populated.** The generator covers all 471 names by construction. That is record coverage. The attention assessment covers none of them with a measurement. That is zero measured attention coverage. These results coexist without contradiction. Calling the first comprehensive cannot increase the second.

**A repair that changes only future instructions leaves the producing mechanism intact until uptake occurs.** Saving a new selection rule is a real repository change. It is not evidence that a later model made a better choice. The [mind-change case, line 5](https://github.com/benjam3n/subjectsystems/blob/55364748b120126cb9e8126cc685838f3c8409c3/cases/mind-change-selection.md#L5) correctly acknowledges that future uptake remains unobserved. Its value cannot be inflated into demonstrated behavioral change.

**Performativity here is a mismatch between the sign of accomplishment and its actual contribution.** A large catalog, named technique, “Result” column, PASS report, and anti-performativity warning can all function as signs of rigor. Some also perform useful work. They become performative when those signs earn the status that should require the missing result. This definition does not require deliberate deception; the generator can create the mismatch mechanically.

**Softness has two distinct forms.** Claim-specific qualification protects valid inference. Global retreat into definitions, proposed tests, and “can” statements can avoid every consequential verdict while leaving the user’s question unresolved. The first is rigor; the second can be an evasion. In this repository the phrenology case gives a concrete avoidable non-verdict. The finite examples supply counterevidence to any claim that the whole repository refuses hard conclusions: they reject universal sufficiency of unordered component lists, demonstrate a missing GOSM gate branch, and refute mandatory rejection proportions.

**Forced harshness is also performative.** The preserved original ARAW source requests a rejection or uncertainty proportion. The local analysis constructs five valid analytical cases, so an additional forced negative verdict cannot follow from their proofs. A fixed rejection quota optimizes the appearance of toughness and can damage truth. The required standard is to take every warranted consequence, including a strong negative one, without manufacturing either reassurance or condemnation.

**The minimum sufficient prevention is an obligation-preserving transition.** Whenever a description is transformed into another artifact, the operation must preserve whether each item is a question, required output, proposal, actual result, or unresolved remainder. Whenever completion is asserted, the required contribution must have an actual instance or an explicitly established impossibility/availability boundary. A further instruction cannot satisfy that predicate. This is not an optional style rule: without it, the transition changes the claim.

That last principle is applied here: source and generated occurrences are linked; proposals and real findings receive different judgments; the runnable paths were exercised; the retrievable phrenology result was retrieved; and the audit does not turn full-file acquisition into a claim of infallible semantic exhaustiveness.

The user’s demand is best understood as removing the obligation to complete the model’s intellectual work. That includes supplying the relevant distinctions, performing available comparisons, issuing the conclusions the grounds warrant, and arranging them so the user need not reconstruct the argument from a set of labels. This audit shows why producing more correctly named subjects can still fail that demand: the model can improve the description of the work owed while leaving the work itself unpaid.
