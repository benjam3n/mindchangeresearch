## Organization decision

Three arrangements address different uses. Archive-only addition preserves the conversation but leaves ordinary retrieval dependent on extraction. A single expanded inventory preserves all relationships on one page but requires navigating roughly 33,600 words for a particular target. Subject pages with stable IDs return the target and its conditions together; construction and system views expose the operation required for use. The current subject view is adopted for those tasks. Source order and execution accounting remain available where provenance or remaining obligations are the live question.

This is a concrete dependency comparison, not a timed experiment or proof of globally optimal navigation. The change can be assessed immediately by requesting target `368`, `AI-1`, the consequence-construction operation, or the compiler’s implementation gap and inspecting the actual destinations.

## Remaining construction

Most target-specific contributors remain candidates rather than complete recipes. Further work should supply the missing operation for consequential cases, including non-epistemic targets; connect outputs to actual consumers; retain conditions for reuse; and use the relevant subject system wherever an identified weakness is live. Human experience, independent ability, retention, transfer, and actual efficiency need evidence appropriate to those particular effects. These open questions do not prevent use of already supported distinctions or execution of already available operations.

Actual mind change: The project now treats the live missing contribution as the organizing relation. A completed target map, a selection card, a recipe, a performed operation, and an observed effect have distinct standing. The repair changes the current access paths and the generator that will produce later checkpoints.

Benefit: The implemented structure removes an extraction prerequisite for its linked current material, supplies target-specific contributors directly, prevents the known generator from restoring the former index, and retracts an unsupported implementation-completeness claim. Recipient benefit, navigation time, and enduring transfer are not inferred from these repository changes.

Verdict: KEEP for the implemented repository corrections after the integration checks; candidate causal relationships and general recipe efficacy remain unresolved at their stated scopes. No additional allocation or discovery credit is assigned.
