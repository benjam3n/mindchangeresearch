Adapted operation: capture the needed authorized information by t=6, beginning no later than 4, then use it at 10. The literal demand-time acquisition starts at 10 and fails the access constraint. Acquiring everything immediately at 0 is not forced: a selective acquisition during 4…6 retains the JIT concern while satisfying the closing window.
