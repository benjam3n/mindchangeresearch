Intended mind change: Change a work queue when shorter average waiting conflicts with completing a long consequential task before its deadline.

Starting working judgment: Shortest-job-first looked attractive for clearing a mixed backlog quickly. I had not evaluated whether that queue discipline preserved a deadline on the longest job.

Original source: ../sources/conditions-qs.original.md. Exact stdout and separate requirements receipt: ../sources/conditions-qs.requirements.txt; byte checks are in source-receipts.json.

Execution scope and depth: No numerical 8x floor exists. Four disciplines, eight condition checks, implemented selected order and explicit monitoring measures were produced.

Concrete finite queue: A takes 9 units and is due at 10; B takes 1, C takes 3, D takes 2 and are all due at 100. All arrive at time 0, one nonpreemptive processor is available, and setup costs are zero. Units are declared scenario units, not measured human minutes. Input and outputs are in cases.json and case-results.json.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FIFO in declared arrival tie order | A,B,C,D | 0,9,10,13 | 8 | 0 |
| Shortest job first | B,D,C,A | 0,1,3,6 | 2.5 | 1: A finishes 15 |
| Deadline priority, SJF among remaining | A,B,D,C | 0,9,10,12 | 7.75 | 0 |

The optimization goal has two candidate meanings: minimize average delay to many outputs, or preserve A’s deadline while improving delay where possible. For the second, the computed SJF route fails a hard condition; the third route improves the remaining ready-job order after A. No sum of short-job gains makes A finish before 10 in the SJF result.

Eight condition checks: if A has no early deadline, SJF is allowed; if A’s deadline is 15, equality suffices; at 14 it fails; if A is divisible, preemption may change the feasible schedules; if setup between jobs is nonzero, the listed means change; if a job arrives later, fixed initial sorting is insufficient; if jobs have unequal value, job count alone is inadequate; if a long job keeps being postponed by arrivals, add an age limit or protected slot.

Implemented discipline for the declared deadline objective: A first, then B,D,C. Monitoring quantities are start wait, finish time, lateness, and whether a job is repeatedly skipped. The record computes real outputs of the declared queue rather than asserting a universal human productivity effect. Finite completion proves no infinite-horizon anti-starvation property.

Actual mind change: The conditions agent changed the queue from SJF as a default to deadline-first admission with SJF only inside the remaining feasible set.

Benefit: The new finite schedule keeps all declared deadlines and lowers mean wait from the supplied FIFO tie order by 0.25 units. It sacrifices SJF’s lower mean wait to preserve the stated deadline.

Verdict: KEEP

Organization: A table showing both average wait and the missed deadline exposes a trade-off that a “tasks cleared” list hides. The relevant organization is by scheduling objective and constraints.
