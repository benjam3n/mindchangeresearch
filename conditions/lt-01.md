Source structure: a constrained resource limits completion of multiple jobs; idle capacity can delay output. Examples vary: one processor serving queued jobs, one writer replacing a shared file, one device presenting an audio stream, and one person using a room during an access window. The stable relation is contention for a resource; the assumption that all idle time is waste is not stable, as qs-02’s known future release case already demonstrates.

Target domain: a person must understand a dense paragraph while an audio explanation is available. Entities are the paragraph, spoken stream, person, device, pauses, questions and later use. Source processor→person is an analogy, not an identity. Source job completion→human understanding is not a supplied measurable equivalence. Source idle time→silence could support a question, rest, rehearsal or nothing; those are distinct target processes.
