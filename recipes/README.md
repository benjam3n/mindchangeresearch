# Ways to bring about a change

Use these alongside [contribution construction](../mind-change/contribution-construction.md) and the [required Subject Systems](../mind-change/subject-systems.md). Existing applicable operations may be reused with their conditions.

These are scoped operations, worked examples and designs. They are not a universally sufficient sequence or a closed inventory. A current concern may suggest an entry; the attempt may change what the concern is.

| When this describes the situation | Route | What has actually been established |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Several reports may repeat one event | [Recover evidence lineage](evidence-lineage.md) | Exact arithmetic and decision changes in a constructed case; empirical likelihoods are supplied, not learned. |
| Information exists but the needed exception is hard to retrieve | [Change the access arrangement](access-and-coordination.md) | An existing finite case preserves 48 fields and changes bounded retrieval work; durable learning is untested. |
| Someone accepts the evidence but the action still does not occur | [Locate the missing condition](access-and-coordination.md) | The supplied access case supports an authorized coordination route; no publication receipt is fabricated. |
| Waiting for understanding may destroy an option | [Preserve or release an option](timing-and-options.md) | Exact policy boundaries under stated payoffs and timing; no universal preference for waiting or reservation. |
| A correct account keeps determining which next steps are thinkable | [Depart from a correct account](leave-a-correct-account.md) | A worked counterexample to the sufficiency of true propositions; general transformative efficacy is untested. |
| Something cannot yet be expressed in the old categories | [Allow a distinction to take form](formation-before-comparison.md) | A concrete elicitation design and a simulated relation case; no unknown meaning is declared understood. |
| The criterion or the subject may constitute the wrong undertaking | [Change criteria and subjects](revise-criteria-and-subjects.md) | Exact distinctions and worked comparisons; authority over human ends is not manufactured. |

The [cycle-03 compiler](../post-allocation/cycle-03/consolidation-03-recipe-compiler.md) remains useful when the change can already be specified well enough to select a capable operation. Its tests cover a finite set of cases. Its schema is not an algorithm for discovering every valid mechanism.

For an established guarantee, name sufficient conditions and derive the promised result under them. For an empirical reliability claim, provide relevant observations and an uncertainty estimate. For a proposed route, say what is designed, what is missing and what would constitute an informative attempt. Do not turn the absence of a universal guarantee into a reason to stop constructing stronger recipes.

Executing one of these pages does not by itself complete an original Reasoningtool skill. Selected originals still require their actual sources and operations. Reusing an established finding does not create a new distinct KEEP.
