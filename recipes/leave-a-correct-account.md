# Depart from an account without first making it false

This is a route design with a worked logical example, not a proved general method of transformation. Use when a strong, correct account keeps defining which continuations deserve consideration.

## Operations to attempt

1. State the strongest continuation you would actually pursue. Identify what makes it seem appropriate: perhaps every proposal must improve a score, preserve a vocabulary, answer the original question or remain recognizable as an argument.
2. Choose one such requirement and temporarily withhold its authority over what may be explored. Do not weaken the old account or claim its opposite is true. Actual permissions and available capabilities still govern actions.
3. Encounter the matter through a route that does not require that admission ticket: participate in an activity, examine an excluded instance, change a representation, listen to another person's account, or try a different available arrangement. Name the concrete operation; do not substitute “think differently.”
4. Develop what becomes distinguishable before requiring a translation into the old account. If nothing becomes intelligible, retain that result without calling it proof that nothing exists.
5. When a claim or action is proposed, ask what supports it and where the old findings still apply. Do not demand preservation of every old accomplishment unless the new account claims to preserve it.

These operations are possible moves, not a mandatory sequence. The encounter may precede the recognition of the withheld requirement.

## Worked example: hours and coordination

Suppose a team has two members, each available for four hours. In arrangement A both work 09:00–13:00. In arrangement B one works 09:00–13:00 and the other 13:00–17:00. Each member's reported hours and the total of eight person-hours are true in both arrangements.

A strong account of total staffing can compare cost and total available labor correctly. It cannot, from those totals alone, answer how much simultaneous work is available. Withholding the requirement that every useful explanation be stated in individual-hour totals makes overlap available as a new relation: four hours in A and zero in B.

If the undertaking requires simultaneous collaboration, the relation changes the question and possible arrangements. If it requires consecutive coverage, B may instead be useful. The totals are not refuted. Nor has overlap earned universal authority over all staffing questions. What counts as the relevant undertaking can remain unsettled.

The example establishes a precise point: a true projection can omit distinctions needed for another inquiry. It does not show that all inadequacy is projection loss, that the user should adopt a coordination criterion, or that every departure can be guaranteed by this procedure.

## A harder boundary

If no relevant difference can currently be expressed or encountered, do not relabel two accounts as equivalent and eliminate one. Also do not declare an ineffable difference established. Distinctness, importance and eventual intelligibility remain open. A later encounter can alter the available means of discrimination.
