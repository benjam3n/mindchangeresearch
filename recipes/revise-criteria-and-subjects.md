# Change what is being judged and what counts as one thing

Use when the current measure or division into subjects may be constituting the wrong undertaking. This route preserves accountable comparisons without making a shared measure the entrance requirement for every inquiry.

## Route A: examine a criterion's authority

1. Preserve the current judgment under its actual criterion C0. Record what C0 notices and what it treats as irrelevant.
2. Find an occurrence, excluded person, consequence or possibility that puts that authority in question. A different score alone does not explain why a new criterion deserves attention.
3. Develop the candidate criterion C1, allowing the relevant actors, outcomes and horizon to change. Preserve disagreement about what matters; do not invent stakeholders' agreement.
4. Where comparisons are meaningful, show both C0 and C1 results. Explain the proposed change in authority. If no accepted ranking between the criteria exists, leave that ranking unresolved while making the alternatives and their consequences accessible.
5. Try an available, authorized use of the new account that could reveal a relevant omission. The new account's own unexplained exclusions can reopen it.

Example: two designs have throughput scores A=10 and B=8, and recovery scores A=2 and B=6. Maximizing throughput ranks A first; maximizing recovery ranks B first. This arithmetic establishes a ranking reversal. It does not establish that recovery should govern, nor that B improved throughput. If a newly recognized reliance on recovery changes the undertaking, that encounter and its significance must be examined rather than replaced by the score table.

## Route B: let a relation change the subjects

Start with occurrences and the relations that matter to the activity. Compare two arrangements that the old per-subject summaries identify as the same. Ask whether participation in the relation changes which unit can explain or enable the activity.

In the [hours case](leave-a-correct-account.md), individual workers with four hours each do not fully specify a joint working period. The inquiry may need a period of joint availability as a unit. This does not make workers unreal or abolish questions about individual labor. It changes which unit can carry the explanation of this undertaking.

For the research itself, one record may concern time, memory, social coordination and criterion revision. Give it multiple entrances. If those labels obscure what happened, add an account beginning with the occurrence and allow later organization to change the labels. Do not create another mandatory category called “uncategorizable.”

## Outcome and boundary

These examples establish that criterion substitution and subject reconstitution differ from improving a score on a fixed object. They do not supply a universal judge of better criteria. The [existing privacy/auditability case](../post-allocation/cycle-02/values/01-vcd-privacy-auditability.md) supplies a concrete instance where separate conditions cannot be silently traded through an invented common weight; it does not prove that every value relation is permanently noncompensatory.
