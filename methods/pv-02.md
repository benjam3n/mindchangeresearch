Applied repair, not a proposal: single-step-interface-v2.json adds paused, UNREADABLE→paused and re-observation transitions. Paused→PASS reaches start; paused→FAIL reaches connect; paused→UNREADABLE remains paused. A setup_changed event invalidates the previous observation context and returns to connect. The artifact states that physical or human success has not been observed.

Output trace: gathering produces a setup-start state, connecting/selecting/powering produce the authored pre-check state, reading produces either a value or its absence, PASS produces start, absent information produces a pause. Therefore the goal of truthful current instruction is met in the symbolic domain; eventual setup completion is conditional on a readable successful check.
