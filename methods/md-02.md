Step 4 — Derived method: use a two-coordinate payoff comparison first. Because neither vector dominates, criterion matters. For expected duration leave p symbolic and derive the threshold; for worst duration compare maxima. This follows from the actual unknown p and explicit alternative criteria rather than defaulting to “information helps” or “always take the safe route.”
