# A streak cannot be protected at every cost

Application: 071-hf-2. Actor: this assistant's current working model and generated artifacts unless a constructed case is explicitly identified. Human outcomes are not observed.

Expanded scope is stated rather than inventing an 8x certification. Seven formation/breaking steps, eight explicit cost countercases, a mock three-opportunity log and a later record.

Constructed break-habit target: a learner automatically records a completion mark even when the intended inquiry did not occur, because the visible streak is about to break. Underlying motivation: retain an appearance of consistency. This is a stipulated scenario, not a claim about the user.

Four laws for breaking: cue—hide the streak total while the session is underway; craving—distinguish actual inquiry from a satisfying mark; response—require naming the concrete comparison before a full-completion mark; reward—let the record show partial or missed without resetting the value of previous work. No shame, payment, or punishment is proposed. Environment: record fields are “attempted,” “gateway,” and “comparison,” with previous substantive outputs accessible.

Small replacement: write one truthful state, including “not attempted.” This is record repair, not the desired inquiry itself. Tracking: report what happened, do not redefine full inquiry to guarantee a streak. Accountability: voluntary self-inspection; social pressure is not added.

Eight costs that the absolute rule would permit by its words: false completion; deleting counterevidence to finish; violating an agreed commitment; continuing after the actor has chosen to stop; replacing the original goal with a trivial token; hiding missed opportunities; penalizing legitimate context changes; suppressing exploration that does not fit the streak. Each conflicts with an explicit relevant value in the constructed design or the supplied program.

Concrete execution: a mock log has day 1 full comparison, day 2 gateway only, day 3 skipped by choice. The honest record remains [full, gateway, skipped], not [full, full, full]. The first full comparison remains available; no claim that a missed interval erases learning is made.
