# Trust dimensions change a concrete delegation

Application: 026-trust-1. Actor: this assistant's current working model and generated artifacts unless a constructed case is explicitly identified. Human outcomes are not observed.

Starting working judgment: I initially favored a medium overall reliance posture for the fictional analyst because accurate work and a late delivery offset each other in a single summary.

Source: ../sources/values-trust.md. Requirements receipt: ../sources/values-trust.receipt.txt. Original loaded through read_original.py; byte checks are recorded separately in source-integrity.json.

Depth: Original 8x floors: 6+ party pairs, 3 dimensions plus structural and system analysis, 12 events, 10 interventions. Executed: six unordered pairs in both directions, twelve events, ten interventions, each with costs/prerequisites and a scoped signal assessment.

Interpretation: assessment and allocation. Entire social situation is constructed. Each fact below is stipulated; trust levels are conditional reliance assessments generated from those facts, not measurements of a human’s feelings. H/M/L/U mean high/medium/low/unknown within the stated task. No broad allegation of reciprocal blame is imported from the source. The corruption-pre-inoculation original was also loaded and retained.

Asymmetry and history: B’s confidence in A’s calculation is stronger than A’s confidence in B’s claim editing. The late draft affects schedule reliance without undoing the displayed correct calculation. E3/E4 and E5/E6 show local repair sequences; they do not establish a long-term rising trajectory.

System analysis: Incentives favor concise confident outputs, while the task needs uncertainty preserved. Commitments become trackable through dated changes; claim revisions become visible through edit records. D can approve or reject, so A/B need a way to state a missing premise without pretending confidence. The system makes bounded reliance rational only partially: calculation verification is available, future timeliness remains uncertain.

Repair/design sequence: First preserve E3’s exact qualifier correction and E5’s deadline omission. Next complete one bounded calculation and one acknowledged handoff. At the next three handoffs, examine whether the respective commitments held; change reliance only on the observed dimension. The checkpoints are proposed opportunities to observe commitments, not a forecast that a person’s trust will recover in a fixed number of days. Actual trust change would require observed recipient reliance, disclosures, or reports across later interactions; none exists in this constructed application.

Uncomfortable case contribution: D’s wish for a quick confident answer can create pressure that D then interprets as an author’s unreliability. The case therefore needs visible criteria, not only better author conduct. This concerns the fictional party’s role, not a discovered trait of the real user. Confidence is moderate for the map’s logical fit to stipulated facts and low for any reaction prediction. Weakest assumption: how parties interpret the same event; their actual perspectives were not collected.

Source-claim boundary: this application does not assume that violations always outweigh trust-building, that erosion is always bidirectional, or that every integrity breach should be answered by over-delivery. The original research on response to alleged competence and integrity violations examined different conditions; it does not license false denial or a universal repair rule. [Kim, Ferrin, Cooper and Dirks, primary study](https://ink.library.smu.edu.sg/lkcsb_research/2368/)

Certificate: the dimensional assessment depends on event-specific evidence; a favorable event on competence does not supply an integrity commitment, and an unknown benevolence judgment is not a negative finding. The strongest alternative is that a hidden event changes the map, so the resulting allocation remains scoped to this case.

Subsequent application, executed: New constructed delegation: analyst P has two correct calculations and one late report; analyst Q has on-time reports but an uncorrected denominator error. An overall “both medium” summary leaves both interchangeable. Applying the dimensional map produces P for arithmetic review and Q for a deadline reminder only, with a separate verifier for Q’s calculation. The generated allocation now matches the provided task evidence and avoids assigning arithmetic to the known incorrect calculator.

Actual mind change: The working reliance judgment now separates demonstrated task competence from future commitment reliability; one medium score no longer makes unlike risks interchangeable.

Benefit: The subsequent allocation assigns the calculation to the actor with direct calculation evidence and treats scheduling as a separate responsibility. Human trust repair is unobserved.

Verdict: KEEP

Organization: The content preserves unknown dimensions and unilateral as well as reciprocal failure possibilities. The directional table retains risk and system exposure beside each assessment; intervention plans are distinct from observed outcomes.
