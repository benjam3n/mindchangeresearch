Intended mind change: Turn the gateway intuition into a falsifiable experiment that preserves both voluntary initiation and interest rather than counting starts alone.

# Original /exd dependency: voluntary inquiry gateway

Source: ../sources/values-exd.md; separate receipt: ../sources/values-exd.receipt.txt. Interpretation: design a new experiment. The original defines no numerical 8x scale; all seven steps are supplied with expanded variables, controls, validity threats and power assumptions. No people are recruited and no intervention is deployed.

Starting judgment: a self-chosen two-minute entry looks promising, but the effect on interest and substantive inquiry is unobserved. This design is not a substitute for the program’s broad goal or evidence of a formed habit.

Hypotheses fixed before any participant data: H1, in eligible voluntary inquiry sessions, a two-minute gateway increases substantive starts while intrinsic-interest change is not negative. H0 for the conjunction: either starts do not increase or interest decreases. The gateway is “write one concrete example about your own selected question,” followed by a genuine option to continue or stop. Active control has the same selected question and voluntary continuation but no prescribed entry routine. A rise in mere document openings does not meet the start criterion.

A stronger observable supporting pattern is positive lower confidence bounds for both starts and interest. This would support the conjunction without allowing a small interest loss. A nonsignificant interest difference would remain unresolved; it would not prove equality or no reduction. Exact equality at a zero true effect cannot be certified merely by failing to reject a loss.

Variables: IV gateway versus equally optional active control. Primary DV1 is each participant’s proportion of accessible offered sessions containing at least one concrete example, coded from the actual work. Primary DV2 is the participant’s stated inherent interest in the inquiry, measured at baseline and after the defined session series; the exact measure needs validated scoring or a pilot and is not assumed reliable here. Secondary DVs: voluntary continuation, completed example/counterexample comparisons, chosen stopping, and perceived obligation. Manipulation check: the prompt delivered, gateway attempted, and option to decline actually available. No engagement is inferred from compliance alone.

Control variables: chosen question is held constant within the person; both conditions have equivalent tools, time availability and contact; instructions specify the same freedom to stop. Candidate confounds: initial interest, skill, novelty, expected evaluation, access failure, and differences in available time. Moderators are initial interest and task familiarity, exploratory unless powered separately. No mediator is claimed; reduced setup friction and perceived obligation are candidate mechanisms requiring distinct measures.

Design: parallel two-arm randomized comparison, with randomization blocked by initial interest and task familiarity. A within-person crossover is rejected as primary because learning the gateway can carry into control. Factorial variants crossing gateway and evaluation pressure would test a different, broader experiment; they are retained as future alternatives rather than silently added. Assignment is generated by an independent schedule before invitation wording is administered; concealment and blinded outcome coding are proposed, not performed. Participants cannot be fully blinded to the prompt difference.

Controls compared: no-intervention leaves contact unequal; waitlist delays but does not control expectancy; a generic encouragement prompt adds a different motivational ingredient; equally optional active control best isolates the entry structure. Standard instructions and accessible materials are prerequisites. No deception or compulsory continuation is needed for the stated comparison.

Power calculation actually executed and retained in dependency-ig-2-exd-power.json. Normal approximation for two equal independent groups uses n = ceiling(2(z_0.975 + z_0.90)^2 / d^2). A planning standardized start effect of 0.40 requires 132 complete participants per arm; a stronger positive-interest effect of 0.25 requires 337. Taking 337 and allowing 15% attrition yields 397 invited per arm, 794 total. These are chosen planning effects with unit standardized variance, not estimates from this actor or a claim of exact achieved power. Each endpoint targets 90% power under its stated alternative; the probability both succeed is at least 80% by the union bound under those assumptions. Unknown variance, outcome reliability and bounded rate distributions require pilot-informed recalculation. If the feasible sample is much smaller, the result is a pilot with uncertainty, not a powered confirmation.

Validity threats and specific handling:

1. Selection: random assignment after eligibility and consent; actual implementation absent.
2. Baseline imbalance: report initial interest and skill without selectively redefining arms.
3. History: collect conditions over overlapping calendar periods.
4. Maturation: equal observation duration; no long-term habit claim.
5. Repeated measurement: both arms answer the same measures; test sensitization remains possible.
6. Instrumentation: fixed rubric and measurement timing; actual reliability needs data.
7. Regression: do not enroll only extreme low-interest cases and call rebound an effect.
8. Attrition: report withdrawal by arm; no fictional interest scores after withdrawal.
9. Diffusion: record control participants using a learned gateway; analyze as a deviation, not conceal it.
10. Expectancy: equal contact and neutral claims; participant awareness measured if practical.
11. Demand: do not reward favorable interest reports or more starts.
12. Experimenter bias: blind substantive-work coding where wording permits.
13. Population transfer: the eligible volunteers do not represent all people or all inquiry.
14. Task transfer: a concrete-example gateway need not transfer to artistic or relational goals.
15. Temporal transfer: a session series does not establish lasting habit automaticity.
16. Construct validity: starts, continued inquiry and interest remain separate.
17. Mono-method bias: work products plus reported interest measure different endpoints; neither substitutes for the other.
18. Statistical assumptions: bounded rates and repeated opportunities require appropriate participant-level or hierarchical sensitivity models.
19. Multiple outcomes: both co-primary endpoints must support the conjunction; exploratory endpoints are labeled.
20. Missing values: no completion-only analysis that removes refusals differently by arm.

Analysis plan: intention-to-treat at participant level, with accessible-opportunity definition fixed before data; show raw group rates and interest changes with intervals. A prespecified regression can adjust for blocking factors; robustness analysis uses an appropriate model for bounded rates. Human construct reliability must be checked before treating the interest estimate as decisive. Missing-data sensitivity includes conservative bounds and explicit dropout summaries rather than an assumed improvement. Any post hoc subgroup discovery remains exploratory. No public preregistration is created; this file is the dated proposed analysis plan before any data.

Actual local measurement application: mock events “offered but inaccessible,” “accessible and declined,” “example written,” and “only document opened” become respectively no accessible opportunity, declined opportunity, substantive start, and gateway preparation only. That is an executed four-case classification. It does not test the causal hypothesis about humans.

Actual mind change: The experiment now requires the two conjuncts of the intuition and a real substantive-start definition. It no longer treats a nonsignificant interest difference as proof of no reduction.

Benefit: The proposed test can return unresolved or conflicting evidence instead of manufacturing a motivational success from a convenient proxy.

Verdict: UNRESOLVED

Organization: The power inputs, two outcomes, access denominator and missing human data remain adjacent. The design’s completed status is distinct from an unperformed trial and from the broad mind-change goal.

Next attempts: Pilot the actual measures with willing participants if a real study is later authorized; estimate variance and coding reliability; compare a different entry behavior without altering the outcome definitions after seeing results.
