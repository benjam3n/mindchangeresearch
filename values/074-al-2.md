# A conversation about disagreement needs room for an answer

Application: 074-al-2. Actor: this assistant's current working model and generated artifacts unless a constructed case is explicitly identified. Human outcomes are not observed.

Starting working judgment: A prepared list of good open questions seems sufficient preparation for a fictional conversation about why a participant stopped attending a group.

Source: ../sources/values-al.md. Requirements receipt: ../sources/values-al.receipt.txt. Original loaded through read_original.py; byte checks are recorded separately in source-integrity.json.

Depth: The original specifies no numerical 8x scaling. All presently executable stages are addressed; unavailable live stages are recorded separately. Expanded scope is stated rather than inventing an 8x certification. Nine listening steps, eight distinct answer-dependent branches, three prepared questions, and a concrete closing note.

Interpretation: prepare for a future conversation. Constructed context: a participant has missed two optional reading-group sessions. The organizer wants to understand whether format, access, or interest changed. There is no real absent person being analyzed or contacted.

Preparation: suspend the assumption that missed attendance means dissatisfaction. Remove irrelevant agenda items from the proposed conversation. Set aside a proposed 15-minute optional discussion; actual availability is unknown. Questions prepared: “What was different about the last two sessions for you?”; “Which part would you want more or less of?”; “Is there anything about attendance we have misread?”

Safety opening drafted: “There is no obligation to keep attending. I would like to understand whether the format is serving what you wanted.” Confidentiality is not promised beyond an actual ability to keep it; none is specified. Body-language directions are not simulated in text.

Full attention distinctions: content, reported feeling, value, need, and request remain separate. Eight possible responses and actual next questions generated:

| Constructed answer | Next question |
|---|---|
| The time changed | Which times remain possible? |
| I like listening, not preparing | Would a no-preparation role suit you? |
| I cannot access the shared file | Which format can you open? |
| I have lost interest in this topic | Is there another topic, or do you prefer to stop? |
| I felt interrupted | Which moment should we understand? |
| I am fine; two conflicts occurred | Do you want anything changed? |
| I thought attendance had become mandatory | What created that impression? |
| I do not want to discuss it | Understood; no further question required. |

Silence is a live-conversation option, not a timed event performed here. Paraphrase examples change with answer: after inaccessible file, “The issue is access to the material”; after lost interest, “You no longer want this topic.” Neither becomes an assumption before the answer.

Nonverbal cues: none available; if a real conversation occurs, an observed cue would invite a check rather than a motive verdict. Summary: capture the participant’s chosen change or no-change, then ask whether anything was missed. Confirmation remains pending.

Later application: an unsent closing note for the “two schedule conflicts, no change needed” branch is “Thanks; the format can stay as it is, and attendance remains optional.” The initial question-list-only approach had no branch that terminates with no intervention. This is a designed responsive exchange; it has not been delivered and does not demonstrate changed cooperation.

Listening reflection: an actual feeling-heard outcome requires the participant’s response. A complete script cannot supply that evidence. The design’s strongest risk is that even the optional conversation feels like pressure; the no-discussion branch retains a genuine stop.

Actual mind change: The draft conversation now has a no-change and a no-discussion branch, but no actual listening interaction has occurred.

Benefit: The prepared exchange can respond to different stated reasons without assuming dissatisfaction.

Verdict: UNRESOLVED
