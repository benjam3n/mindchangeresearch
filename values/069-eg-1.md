Synthesis: motivational reason, experienced interest, task entry friction, and role-specific attention are separate constructs. The surprising distinction is meaningful-but-unenjoyable work versus interesting-but-instrumentally-useless work; the former is not failed interest and the latter is not failed usefulness by definition. This is a conceptual comparison, not a claim about the user.
