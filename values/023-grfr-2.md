Constructed original goal, verbatim: “Make every collaborator genuinely interested in this project while leaving each fully free to decline interest.” This is not a personal goal attributed to the user.

Obstacle: “every” and “fully free” do not themselves create a logical contradiction—everyone could freely become interested. They do prevent an actor from guaranteeing the outcome across all allowed collaborator states. A stipulated collaborator who values an unrelated project and remains uninterested after a respectful invitation is an allowed state and defeats the universal guarantee.
