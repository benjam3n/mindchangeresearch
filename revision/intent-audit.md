# Where the research misread the request—and what is repaired

Baseline: `07e6af64c65fb682305659b2ec830e21743c0f15`. Before publication, this revision was reconciled with `2a7c6d920bf9e86cc9a247c39927bcce45e4deea`. The concurrent commits had already exposed native paths, corrected the SPG schema claim, preserved 616 contributor entries, added 25 inquiries, and removed the universal fixed-target rule. Those repairs are retained and are not claimed as new discoveries here. The additions here are the supplied recipe catalog, completed deductions and implementations, and their integration. The final parent subsequently advanced to `5069379a00ef05cb29716a5aeea17ad1f01fd4b1`; its 6,000 catalog entries, rankings, twenty procedures, and framing corrections are also preserved. See [reconciliation](reconciliation.md).

The central defect was not an absence of broad topics or real findings. It was a failure to carry important findings into later instructions, combined with a tendency to replace the construction of a useful result with specifications for producing or testing one later. This revision repairs those active rules, completes available deductions, and changes the primary organization.

The [current requests](user-request.md) govern this audit. The supplied catalog is preserved [unchanged](../catalog/original/mind-change-science.md); the [pre-revision control files](evidence/pre-revision/README.md) preserve the prior entry point. The initial verified historical checkpoint contains 1,465 files; it is not the full later merged corpus. The [inventory](corpus-inventory.md) covers 432 authored records selected by result markers, with their reported results and next-attempt fields. Those extractions supported a corpus-wide pattern review; the explicit cases below received substantive inspection. This is not an independent replication or a claim that every line of the approximately half-million-word authored corpus has been proved correct.

## What was already right, and must survive the repair

The repository already covered motivation, emotion, values, interest, attention, time, location, capability, and coordination. Claiming it had reduced everything to factual persuasion would misread the evidence. It already contained exact posteriors, reachability certificates, schedules, nonapplication boundaries, rejected repetition, and real changes to its own working artifacts.

It also already distinguished source fidelity, scoped completion, benefit, novelty, and later uptake. The [cycle-03 count audit](../post-allocation/cycle-03/motivation/01-sdc-keep-count.md) reports earlier reductions from twelve proposed keeps to seven new findings and nine to three. This revision does not rediscover that correction as if it were absent. The additional defect is that a known distinction had not sufficiently changed the leading organization and later success language.

Original source procedures and required application counts are not assistant-invented templates to discard. The frozen allocation of 300 applications across 150 original skills, plus ten GOSM runs, remains recorded. This revision is ordinary logical analysis, implementation, and integration; it is not a claimed new execution of those original procedures. Missing dependencies, human observations, and delayed stages are preserved without being used to halt unrelated deductions.

## Distinguishing a result from an instruction

A procedure is a legitimate deliverable when the user asks for a procedure. A study design is a legitimate design result. A null result and a proof of underdetermination can also be substantive results. The error occurs when one is labeled as a different achievement: fields as computation, a plan as a performed transition, a proposal as an observation, or a request to derive a consequence as the consequence itself.

This revision makes those roles explicit. The imported catalog contains candidate assemblies and ingredient evidence. The findings contain completed propositions, counterexamples, and operations. The ledger preserves original execution status. The prospective trial remains a trial design. The current instructions direct future work, but are not the sole evidence that the requested present work happened.

The strongest guarantee available depends on the object. Exact finite model behavior can sometimes be proved. An unobserved person's response cannot be derived from premises compatible with both response and nonresponse. The [reliability derivation](../findings/reliability-and-benefit.md) keeps the ambition intact while identifying the missing empirical links, rather than adding an unsupported guarantee.

## Organization selected after comparison

Twelve perspectives were carried through to concrete consequences in [perspectives-compared.md](perspectives-compared.md). A completed-consequence view is added to the front page alongside the newer inquiry, contributor, target, recipe, and historical views. It does not become a mandatory starting point. This preserves different uses instead of pretending one taxonomy answers every question. The comparison does not establish measured navigation speed or a globally optimal organization.
