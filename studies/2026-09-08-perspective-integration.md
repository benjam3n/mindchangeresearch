Intended mind change: Make the user's inquiry capable of changing how this research proceeds, and make the preserved work accessible through the changes and questions it addresses.

Actual starting judgment: The latest complete checkpoint preserved the corpus, and the current finite transition compiler could govern future inquiry. Inspection supported the preservation claim but exposed inaccessible native paths and an unjustified extension of the compiler's jurisdiction.

A single universal recipe interface was rejected as the governing organization because it presupposes settled targets in the very cases that question them.

## Persistence correction

The old generator could replace current conceptual navigation with its hardcoded quota-first index and absolute scratch links. The replacement builds a separate progress view, validates accepted ledger hashes, and packages all corpus bytes, including empty and binary files. It does not infer research statuses. Transport parts are excluded from the corpus archive to prevent recursive packaging and verified through independent per-part and aggregate hashes.

The saved continuation prompt starts from current repository instructions. An independently configured live automation was not inspected or modified by this edit.

## Validation scope

The reproducible checks verify imported artifact hashes; all 25 source extracts and their 373 entries; the vocabulary constraint inside inquiry 7; current navigation; the 300 recorded application hashes; exact confidence calculations, timing boundaries, overlap distinction and criterion reversal; and full archive round-trip equality. [Integration verification](2026-09-08-integration-verification.json) records results and the distinction between preserved and deliberately replaced baseline files.

Actual mind change: The compiler is retained as a scoped route and loses its universal entrance authority. The repository can now host inquiry in which the concern, subject, alternatives and criteria are among what changes.

Benefit: Practical and foundational entry points now resolve directly to native records; the complete source and unfinished obligations remain recoverable. This establishes a repository repair and local interpretive change, not observed human transformation, general efficiency or perfection.

Verdict: Maintenance repair adopted after validation; no new counted KEEP finding or original-skill application.

Organization: Multiple revisable entrances, exact source history, scoped evidence and separate progress accounting.

Next attempts: Perform a genuinely new use of a foundational route; develop and evaluate concrete affective, perceptual, interest or embodied routes when the necessary inputs are available; compare achieved benefits and effort against strong alternatives; resume ready unfinished original stages.
