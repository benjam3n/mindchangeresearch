# Emotion and appraisal

[All targets and attempts](../contents.md) · [Status and evidence](../README.md)

These are dimensions to consider, not destinations that every inquiry must fix in advance. Each procedure below is an imported candidate outline; its checks describe the intended outcome rather than an observed result of this revision.

<a id="t37"></a>
## T37: Attraction or aversion

**Candidate improvement:** Attraction or aversion better reflects actual consequences and chosen values.

**Procedure outline:** Identify what the response attaches to; separate observed consequences from added interpretation; compare credible alternatives or representative experiences where appropriate; update the appraisal only as evidence supports.

**Different recipes:** [A75a: Separate event from appraisal](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a75a); [A49a: Use a real trade-off](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a49a); [A41c: Replace an irrelevant task](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a41c)

**Outcome check:** The person's own reported appraisal or voluntary choice changes without denying real costs. These ordinary communication routes are not established treatments for entrenched aversion.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S17](../sources.md#s17)

<a id="t38"></a>
## T38: Sense of urgency

**Candidate improvement:** Urgency matches genuine timing constraints and consequences of delay.

**Procedure outline:** Verify any expiration or accumulating cost; identify what must happen before it; distinguish a final decision from an intermediate checkpoint; choose the next feasible time-sensitive action.

**Different recipes:** [A57a: Honor a real expiration](../attempts/decision-and-action.md#a57a); [A57b: Bound inquiry](../attempts/decision-and-action.md#a57b); [A57c: Set a checkpoint instead of forcing a verdict](../attempts/decision-and-action.md#a57c)

**Outcome check:** Action occurs in time without avoidable rushed errors. If no meaningful cost of delay exists, reduce urgency rather than manufacturing a deadline.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.

<a id="t39"></a>
## T39: Perceived threat

**Candidate improvement:** Perceived threat tracks supported exposure and consequences, with available responses visible.

**Procedure outline:** State the feared event precisely; separate likelihood, severity, and uncertainty; inspect evidence relevant to actual exposure; pair the supported concern with a feasible proportionate action.

**Different recipes:** [A43a: Pair a hazard with control](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a43a); [A43b: Respond conditionally to uncertainty](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a43b); [A43c: Reduce a false alarm](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a43c)

**Outcome check:** Appraisal becomes more proportionate and action addresses the real concern. If danger is real, change the situation where possible rather than merely lowering the feeling.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.

<a id="t40"></a>
## T40: Perceived safety

**Candidate improvement:** Perceived safety rests on supported limits, controllable conditions, or dependable assistance.

**Procedure outline:** Identify the uncertainty behind the unsafe appraisal; state what evidence does and does not establish; provide a concrete available action or support; preserve unresolved risk explicitly.

**Different recipes:** [A45a: Give supported bounds](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a45a); [A45b: Make the next step controllable](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a45b); [A45c: Offer dependable support](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a45c)

**Outcome check:** The person can distinguish what is secure from what remains uncertain. An unsupported promise of a good outcome fails even if it briefly soothes.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.

<a id="t41"></a>
## T41: Meaning assigned to frustration

**Candidate improvement:** Frustration is interpreted as information about a specific mismatch rather than automatic proof of incapacity or virtue.

**Procedure outline:** Locate the operation where progress stops; inspect knowledge, resources, method, and task demands; identify the supported source of mismatch; change that condition or stop the unworthy task.

**Different recipes:** [A30b: Repair a prerequisite](../attempts/concepts-and-possibilities.md#a30b); [A76c: Review strategy at a boundary](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a76c); [A80a: Repair prerequisite difficulty](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a80a)

**Outcome check:** The interpretation leads to a repair, changed route, or justified stop. If difficulty persists after one repair, update the diagnosis rather than insisting frustration means growth.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S8](../sources.md#s8), [S2](../sources.md#s2)

<a id="t42"></a>
## T42: Tolerance of uncertainty

**Candidate improvement:** Unresolved uncertainty can coexist with proportionate action when further certainty is unavailable or not worth its cost.

**Procedure outline:** Name the uncertainty; identify what decisions it could change; choose an acceptable action robust across plausible states or obtain consequential information; specify when reopening is warranted.

**Different recipes:** [A14b: Perform sensitivity analysis](../attempts/evidence-and-inference.md#a14b); [A59b: Stop when information cannot change the choice](../attempts/decision-and-action.md#a59b); [A77b: Require a reason to reopen](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a77b)

**Outcome check:** The decision no longer waits for irrelevant certainty, while genuine reversal conditions remain active. This is a decision procedure, not a guarantee of emotional comfort.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.
