# Self-correction

[All targets and attempts](../contents.md) · [Status and evidence](../README.md)

These are dimensions to consider, not destinations that every inquiry must fix in advance. Each procedure below is an imported candidate outline; its checks describe the intended outcome rather than an observed result of this revision.

<a id="t67"></a>
## T67: Interpretation of difficulty

**Candidate improvement:** Difficulty is interpreted according to its cause and actual effect on learning or work.

**Procedure outline:** Locate the difficult operation; distinguish missing knowledge, useful practice demand, excessive load, and an unsuitable objective; inspect actual performance; select instruction, practice, simplification, or goal revision accordingly.

**Different recipes:** [A80a: Repair prerequisite difficulty](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a80a); [A80c: Fit challenge to available capacity](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a80c); [A100a: Separate experience from performance](../attempts/optimization-and-evaluation.md#a100a)

**Outcome check:** The interpretation predicts which repair helps. Neither ease nor struggle by itself establishes learning or incapacity.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S1](../sources.md#s1), [S2](../sources.md#s2), [S8](../sources.md#s8)

<a id="t68"></a>
## T68: Recognition of an error

**Candidate improvement:** Consequential errors are detected and distinguished from uncertainty or disagreement about standards.

**Procedure outline:** Specify the external standard or intended result; compare the actual output; locate the first material mismatch; determine whether the cause is an error, missing evidence, or a disputed criterion.

**Different recipes:** [A66a: Repair the controlling error](../attempts/learning-and-memory.md#a66a); [A35b: Repair the generating rule](../attempts/trust-and-social-relations.md#a35b); [A99a: Anchor revisions externally](../attempts/optimization-and-evaluation.md#a99a)

**Outcome check:** A real error is identified accurately and repaired. If the benchmark is wrong or unclear, fix it rather than rewarding agreement with it.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.

<a id="t69"></a>
## T69: Choice of reasoning strategy

**Candidate improvement:** The reasoning strategy fits the operation needed to resolve the present problem.

**Procedure outline:** Classify the missing operation as calculation, causal inference, comparison, retrieval, or generation; check the strategy's prerequisites; select a matching procedure; change strategy when a specific validity condition fails.

**Different recipes:** [A30a: Distinguish knowing from being able](../attempts/concepts-and-possibilities.md#a30a); [A95c: Recombine components](../attempts/optimization-and-evaluation.md#a95c); [A85a: Choose a table or spatial mapping](../attempts/conversation-and-joint-work.md#a85a)

**Outcome check:** The strategy supplies the required output at justified cost. If effort repeats without new information or inference, change the operation rather than its wording.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.

<a id="t70"></a>
## T70: What gets monitored

**Candidate improvement:** Monitoring focuses on signals that can trigger a useful correction.

**Procedure outline:** Identify a consequential failure mode; select its most informative observable signal; place the check at a natural boundary; attach a specific repair to the signal.

**Different recipes:** [A76a: Monitor a specific warning sign](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a76a); [A76b: Keep a minimal error record](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a76b); [A76c: Review strategy at a boundary](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a76c)

**Outcome check:** The signal detects actionable problems with tolerable false alarms and burden. If nothing changes when it is measured, retire or redesign the measure.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.

<a id="t71"></a>
## T71: Standard used to judge improvement

**Candidate improvement:** The improvement standard measures the valued result separately from appealing proxies.

**Procedure outline:** Specify what should become possible or better; distinguish execution, subjective experience, immediate effect, and durable capability; choose the observation matching that target; preserve important costs in the judgment.

**Different recipes:** [A100a: Separate experience from performance](../attempts/optimization-and-evaluation.md#a100a); [A94b: Observe a decision consequence](../attempts/optimization-and-evaluation.md#a94b); [A21c: Reject a perfectly executed useless aim](../attempts/concepts-and-possibilities.md#a21c)

**Outcome check:** A method can be judged ineffective despite praise or perfect execution, and useful despite little excitement. If the measure rewards a useless outcome, change the target and record why.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S1](../sources.md#s1), [S2](../sources.md#s2), [S15](../sources.md#s15), [S21](../sources.md#s21)

<a id="t72"></a>
## T72: Procedure for revising that standard

**Candidate improvement:** Standards are revised for a defensible change in goals, evidence, or measurement validity, without erasing prior failure.

**Procedure outline:** Record the existing standard and its purpose; identify the new evidence or goal change; test whether the revision still tracks endorsed value; apply the revised standard prospectively while preserving prior results under the old one.

**Different recipes:** [A35b: Repair the generating rule](../attempts/trust-and-social-relations.md#a35b); [A97a: Preserve decision structure](../attempts/optimization-and-evaluation.md#a97a); [A99c: Preserve a recoverable revision history](../attempts/optimization-and-evaluation.md#a99c)

**Outcome check:** The revision improves relevance or validity and cannot simply convert a failed outcome into a claimed success. If only convenience motivates the change, retain the original judgment.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.
