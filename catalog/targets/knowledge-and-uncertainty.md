# Knowledge and uncertainty

[All targets and attempts](../contents.md) · [Status and evidence](../README.md)

These are dimensions to consider, not destinations that every inquiry must fix in advance. Each procedure below is an imported candidate outline; its checks describe the intended outcome rather than an observed result of this revision.

<a id="t1"></a>
## T1: Factual beliefs

**Candidate improvement:** Greater factual accuracy at the relevant scope, including abandoning unsupported certainty.

**Procedure outline:** Isolate the exact proposition; identify the best available direct evidence; replace or narrow only the unsupported part; carry the correction into its consequential inference.

**Different recipes:** [A11a: Correct one factual proposition](../attempts/evidence-and-inference.md#a11a); [A11c: Narrow a partly true claim](../attempts/evidence-and-inference.md#a11c); [A15a: Lead with a discriminating observation](../attempts/evidence-and-inference.md#a15a)

**Outcome check:** A fresh use of the fact is correct and preserves its qualification. If assent changes but use does not, address retrieval or the dependent inference.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S4](../sources.md#s4)

<a id="t2"></a>
## T2: Causal explanations

**Candidate improvement:** An explanation accounts for observations and makes discriminating predictions without invented causal links.

**Procedure outline:** Write the proposed chain of causes; mark observed and inferred links; derive a prediction separating a plausible rival; revise the chain using available discriminating evidence.

**Different recipes:** [A18a: Trace the proposed cause to the outcome](../attempts/evidence-and-inference.md#a18a); [A18b: Compare rival mechanisms](../attempts/evidence-and-inference.md#a18b); [A23b: Test a common cause](../attempts/concepts-and-possibilities.md#a23b)

**Outcome check:** The explanation survives a relevant new observation or is explicitly bounded where no discrimination is available. Fluent storytelling alone is insufficient.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S7](../sources.md#s7)

<a id="t3"></a>
## T3: Predictions

**Candidate improvement:** Forecasts become better calibrated and more discriminating across comparable resolved events.

**Procedure outline:** Define the event and resolution date; start from a relevant reference class; adjust for independent case evidence; record the forecast before resolution and compare it with outcomes.

**Different recipes:** [A28a: Forecast a defined event](../attempts/concepts-and-possibilities.md#a28a); [A28b: Predict from competing mechanisms](../attempts/concepts-and-possibilities.md#a28b); [A46b: Calibrate confidence](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a46b)

**Outcome check:** Evaluate a series of comparable forecasts with an appropriate score and calibration check. One correct prediction does not establish calibration.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S11](../sources.md#s11)

<a id="t4"></a>
## T4: Uncertainty ranges

**Candidate improvement:** The uncertainty range represents defensible possibilities at a stated coverage level or clearly stated qualitative scope.

**Procedure outline:** Define the quantity; distinguish variation from missing knowledge; construct the range from relevant data or explicit assumptions; show whether choices differ across that range.

**Different recipes:** [A14a: Partition the account](../attempts/evidence-and-inference.md#a14a); [A14b: Perform sensitivity analysis](../attempts/evidence-and-inference.md#a14b); [A28c: Find the decision boundary](../attempts/concepts-and-possibilities.md#a28c)

**Outcome check:** Check observed coverage over repeated comparable cases when possible. Otherwise label the range's basis; widen or change the model if it excludes credible possibilities.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S11](../sources.md#s11), [S14](../sources.md#s14)

<a id="t5"></a>
## T5: Confidence in a particular claim

**Candidate improvement:** Confidence moves toward evidential support, upward or downward as warranted.

**Procedure outline:** Separate the claim from how certain it feels; trace the independent evidence; identify its strongest limitation; state the supported confidence and what would change it.

**Different recipes:** [A13b: Separate a strong finding from a tentative explanation](../attempts/evidence-and-inference.md#a13b); [A15b: Combine independent contributions](../attempts/evidence-and-inference.md#a15b); [A77c: Calibrate uncertainty](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a77c)

**Outcome check:** Confidence responds to new evidence rather than repetition or presentation strength. Without repeated resolved cases, personal calibration remains only partly known.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S10](../sources.md#s10), [S11](../sources.md#s11), [S14](../sources.md#s14)

<a id="t6"></a>
## T6: Recognition of what is unknown

**Candidate improvement:** Unknowns are localized so they neither contaminate established knowledge nor disappear behind certainty.

**Procedure outline:** List the conclusion's necessary inputs; mark each established, inferred, or missing; distinguish obtainable missing inputs from presently unresolved ones; pursue only unknowns consequential to the task.

**Different recipes:** [A14c: Separate obtainable and unresolved uncertainty](../attempts/evidence-and-inference.md#a14c); [A2b: Resolve decision-sensitive uncertainty](../attempts/presentation-and-access.md#a2b); [A59b: Stop when information cannot change the choice](../attempts/decision-and-action.md#a59b)

**Outcome check:** The person can state what remains unknown and what can still be concluded or done. If everything becomes doubtful, restore the established premises.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.
