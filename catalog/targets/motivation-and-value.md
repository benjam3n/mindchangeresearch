# Motivation and value

[All targets and attempts](../contents.md) · [Status and evidence](../README.md)

These are dimensions to consider, not destinations that every inquiry must fix in advance. Each procedure below is an imported candidate outline; its checks describe the intended outcome rather than an observed result of this revision.

<a id="t31"></a>
## T31: Goals

**Candidate improvement:** Goals have endorsed value, feasible scope, and a defensible connection to the underlying concern.

**Procedure outline:** Imagine the stated goal fully achieved; identify the benefit that would remain; compare it with costs and competing aims; retain, replace, narrow, or retire the goal on that basis.

**Different recipes:** [A21c: Reject a perfectly executed useless aim](../attempts/concepts-and-possibilities.md#a21c); [A49c: Resolve competing goals](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a49c); [A50b: Choose a purpose](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a50b)

**Outcome check:** The chosen goal is worth pursuing under the person's values even when described without motivational decoration. If its achievement remains useless, change the target.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.

<a id="t32"></a>
## T32: Priorities

**Candidate improvement:** Priorities govern actual resource conflicts instead of remaining an unordered list of good things.

**Procedure outline:** Choose the live conflict; specify each goal's benefit and resource demand; seek a compatible allocation; state the priority or time-bounded compromise where conflict remains.

**Different recipes:** [A49c: Resolve competing goals](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a49c); [A58a: Protect a priority](../attempts/decision-and-action.md#a58a); [A79c: Protect enduring priorities](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a79c)

**Outcome check:** The allocation reflects the endorsed trade-off and can be revised when conditions change. If all goals remain nominally first, the conflict is unresolved.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.

<a id="t33"></a>
## T33: Interest

**Candidate improvement:** Interest grows through an authentic connection, intelligible question, or useful capability.

**Procedure outline:** Identify an already valued concern or unresolved curiosity; connect specific material to it; make the connection usable in a concrete activity; preserve the option that this topic is not worth attention now.

**Different recipes:** [A41a: Generate a personal connection](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a41a); [A41b: Trace a real consequence](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a41b); [A92b: Supply a new operation](../attempts/optimization-and-evaluation.md#a92b)

**Outcome check:** Voluntary engagement or reported interest changes in the relevant activity. If no real connection exists, choose a better topic rather than inventing personal relevance.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S9](../sources.md#s9)

<a id="t34"></a>
## T34: Expected value of success

**Candidate improvement:** Expected value of success reflects plausible benefit, likelihood, and the value of alternatives.

**Procedure outline:** Specify the actual successful outcome; estimate its value in the person's terms; assess likelihood under available conditions; compare the resulting prospect with costs and alternatives without false numerical precision.

**Different recipes:** [A42c: Compare plausible futures](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a42c); [A53c: Locate sensitive judgments](../attempts/decision-and-action.md#a53c); [A49a: Use a real trade-off](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a49a)

**Outcome check:** The revised estimate changes a justified decision or confirms it for stated reasons. If the benefit is attractive but unattainable, improve feasibility or choose another goal.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.

<a id="t35"></a>
## T35: Costs considered acceptable

**Candidate improvement:** Accepted costs reflect explicit trade-offs and genuine constraints rather than pressure or omitted burdens.

**Procedure outline:** List immediate, delayed, and maintenance costs; distinguish unacceptable limits from negotiable preferences; compare feasible alternatives; choose the sacrifice the person endorses.

**Different recipes:** [A53a: Separate constraints from preferences](../attempts/decision-and-action.md#a53a); [A49a: Use a real trade-off](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a49a); [A25c: Match the time scale](../attempts/concepts-and-possibilities.md#a25c)

**Outcome check:** The chosen action remains acceptable with its costs visible. If willingness depends on hiding a burden, the route has not produced a beneficial change.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.

<a id="t36"></a>
## T36: Willingness to expend effort

**Candidate improvement:** Effort is allocated to a worthwhile action that has a plausible path to progress.

**Procedure outline:** Diagnose whether the gap is value, expectancy, resources, or initiation; repair the demonstrated missing condition; define a feasible unit of effort; link continuation to meaningful progress.

**Different recipes:** [A30a: Distinguish knowing from being able](../attempts/concepts-and-possibilities.md#a30a); [A46c: Repair the conditions for success](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a46c); [A54a: Connect a cue to an available action](../attempts/decision-and-action.md#a54a)

**Outcome check:** The person undertakes useful effort at acceptable cost. If effort rises without progress, revise the route; if the goal is not endorsed, reconsider the goal.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S9](../sources.md#s9), [S15](../sources.md#s15), [S23](../sources.md#s23)
