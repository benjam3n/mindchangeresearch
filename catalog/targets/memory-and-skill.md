# Memory and skill

[All targets and attempts](../contents.md) · [Status and evidence](../README.md)

These are dimensions to consider, not destinations that every inquiry must fix in advance. Each procedure below is an imported candidate outline; its checks describe the intended outcome rather than an observed result of this revision.

<a id="t61"></a>
## T61: Accessibility of known information

**Candidate improvement:** Known information can be retrieved at the point of relevant use.

**Procedure outline:** Identify the use condition; attempt retrieval with an appropriate cue; correct missing or inaccurate content from a reliable reference; reconnect the recovered content to a real application.

**Different recipes:** [A62a: Retrieve without the answer](../attempts/learning-and-memory.md#a62a); [A62b: Use decreasing cues](../attempts/learning-and-memory.md#a62b); [A70a: Bind recall to an event](../attempts/learning-and-memory.md#a70a)

**Outcome check:** The information is recovered without unnecessary restudy at a later use point. If recall works but application fails, target recognition or transfer.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S1](../sources.md#s1)

<a id="t62"></a>
## T62: Retention over time

**Candidate improvement:** Correct information remains available after the delay the task actually requires.

**Procedure outline:** Establish correct initial understanding; distribute retrieval across relevant delays; supply corrective feedback; adjust support and revisit intervals to observed recovery.

**Different recipes:** [A63a: Space according to recoverability](../attempts/learning-and-memory.md#a63a); [A62a: Retrieve without the answer](../attempts/learning-and-memory.md#a62a); [A100b: Compare predicted and retained knowledge](../attempts/optimization-and-evaluation.md#a100b)

**Outcome check:** Delayed retrieval meets the intended support level and accuracy. Immediate ease or repeated exposure cannot substitute for this outcome.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S1](../sources.md#s1), [S13](../sources.md#s13)

<a id="t63"></a>
## T63: Associations that cue recall

**Candidate improvement:** Cues recover the intended association and its material qualification rather than a misleading fragment.

**Procedure outline:** Identify the actual retrieval occasion; encode a short accurate link; preserve the discriminating feature or exception; use the cue in a fresh relevant instance.

**Different recipes:** [A70a: Bind recall to an event](../attempts/learning-and-memory.md#a70a); [A70b: Encode a contrast](../attempts/learning-and-memory.md#a70b); [A70c: Keep the qualification attached](../attempts/learning-and-memory.md#a70c)

**Outcome check:** The cue retrieves the correct rule and boundary. If it retrieves only a catchy phrase, rebuild the association around the needed operation.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S1](../sources.md#s1)

<a id="t64"></a>
## T64: Accuracy of a skill

**Candidate improvement:** The skill produces accurate results on representative tasks with the intended level of assistance.

**Procedure outline:** Define the required performance; demonstrate missing operations or locate the first controlling error; repair that operation with appropriate support; perform another representative task.

**Different recipes:** [A61a: Show a worked solution](../attempts/learning-and-memory.md#a61a); [A66a: Repair the controlling error](../attempts/learning-and-memory.md#a66a); [A80a: Repair prerequisite difficulty](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a80a)

**Outcome check:** Accuracy improves on a fresh case, including rule selection. If support supplies the target operation, do not claim independent skill.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S20](../sources.md#s20), [S21](../sources.md#s21)

<a id="t65"></a>
## T65: Fluency of performance

**Candidate improvement:** Performance becomes more fluent without sacrificing accuracy or flexibility.

**Procedure outline:** Establish accurate execution; identify the operation consuming unnecessary effort; practice or externalize it according to the goal; reduce assistance or time only while accuracy remains acceptable.

**Different recipes:** [A69a: Fade worked steps](../attempts/learning-and-memory.md#a69a); [A80b: Increase one demand](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a80b); [A60a: Externalize a difficult representation](../attempts/decision-and-action.md#a60a)

**Outcome check:** Comparable tasks require less time or effort at the required accuracy. If speed rises by skipping essential reasoning, restore that operation.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S20](../sources.md#s20)

<a id="t66"></a>
## T66: Transfer to unfamiliar cases

**Candidate improvement:** The person recognizes and adapts the governing structure in unfamiliar cases.

**Procedure outline:** Compare structurally related examples; name the invariant and its limits; vary surface context or a consequential condition; perform an unfamiliar application at the intended support level.

**Different recipes:** [A64b: Expose an invariant](../attempts/learning-and-memory.md#a64b); [A64c: Practice adaptation](../attempts/learning-and-memory.md#a64c); [A65c: Defeat a misleading resemblance](../attempts/learning-and-memory.md#a65c)

**Outcome check:** A new case is handled correctly with the required adaptation. Familiar repetition alone cannot establish transfer; if transfer fails, isolate recognition from execution.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S12](../sources.md#s12), [S13](../sources.md#s13)
