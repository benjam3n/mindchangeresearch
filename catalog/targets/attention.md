# Attention

[All targets and attempts](../contents.md) · [Status and evidence](../README.md)

These are dimensions to consider, not destinations that every inquiry must fix in advance. Each procedure below is an imported candidate outline; its checks describe the intended outcome rather than an observed result of this revision.

<a id="t13"></a>
## T13: What gets noticed

**Candidate improvement:** Attention detects previously missed information that matters to the task.

**Procedure outline:** Identify a consequential missed signal; contrast a case containing it with one without it; place a cue at the natural encounter point; connect noticing the signal to the relevant response.

**Different recipes:** [A65a: Construct a minimal pair](../attempts/learning-and-memory.md#a65a); [A72a: Introduce a meaningful contrast](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a72a); [A70a: Bind recall to an event](../attempts/learning-and-memory.md#a70a)

**Outcome check:** The signal is noticed in a fresh relevant case without swamping attention with false alarms. If detection works but response fails, change the action route.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.

## T14: What gets ignored

**Candidate improvement:** Irrelevant input receives less attention while consequential exceptions remain visible.

**Procedure outline:** Name the distracting stream; separate information that can change the task from routine noise; suppress or defer the latter; preserve a route for the former.

**Different recipes:** [A73a: Protect a coherent work unit](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a73a); [A73c: Batch routine checks](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a73c); [A72b: Reduce excess stimulation](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a72b)

**Outcome check:** The actual task gains continuity without material missed events. If important information is lost, narrow the filter.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.

## T15: Relative salience

**Candidate improvement:** Salience reflects relevance and consequence rather than vividness alone.

**Procedure outline:** List the factors controlling the outcome; compare their evidential and practical importance; put the decisive factor in a prominent comparable representation; attach vivid examples only within their representative scope.

**Different recipes:** [A24b: Restore an omitted cost](../attempts/concepts-and-possibilities.md#a24b); [A25b: Restore the distribution](../attempts/concepts-and-possibilities.md#a25b); [A85a: Choose a table or spatial mapping](../attempts/conversation-and-joint-work.md#a85a)

**Outcome check:** A choice or explanation gives appropriate weight to the controlling factor. If vivid exceptions dominate, restore base rates and the distribution.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S19](../sources.md#s19)

<a id="t16"></a>
## T16: Duration of attention

**Candidate improvement:** Attention lasts long enough to complete a useful unit and stops when continuation loses value.

**Procedure outline:** Define one coherent work unit; match it to an available interval; remove demonstrated interruptions; finish or checkpoint before reassessing whether to continue.

**Different recipes:** [A73a: Protect a coherent work unit](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a73a); [A71c: Allocate reflection by decision value](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a71c); [A80c: Fit challenge to available capacity](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a80c)

**Outcome check:** Useful work is completed with tolerable cost. Longer sitting alone is not improvement; if progress stalls, inspect prerequisites and task size.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.

<a id="t17"></a>
## T17: Conditions for switching attention

**Candidate improvement:** Switching responds to meaningful completion, invalidation, or urgency rather than every incoming stimulus.

**Procedure outline:** Define the current work boundary; name exceptions that warrant immediate switching; capture nonurgent arrivals; switch at the boundary or when a real exception occurs.

**Different recipes:** [A73b: Capture without switching](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a73b); [A73c: Batch routine checks](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a73c); [A76c: Review strategy at a boundary](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a76c)

**Outcome check:** Necessary switches occur while avoidable fragmentation declines. If the rule delays consequential information, refine the exception.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.

<a id="t18"></a>
## T18: What stays available in the background

**Candidate improvement:** Important background information remains recoverable without continuously occupying attention.

**Procedure outline:** Identify information needed intermittently; encode it in a short accurate external reference; attach a recognizable use cue; retrieve it only when the task reaches that condition.

**Different recipes:** [A70a: Bind recall to an event](../attempts/learning-and-memory.md#a70a); [A97c: Separate core from recoverable detail](../attempts/optimization-and-evaluation.md#a97c); [A60a: Externalize a difficult representation](../attempts/decision-and-action.md#a60a)

**Outcome check:** The information is available at use and does not require constant rehearsal. If cues fail, change their placement; if content is wrong, repair the reference.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.
