# Self and social understanding

[All targets and attempts](../contents.md) · [Status and evidence](../README.md)

These are dimensions to consider, not destinations that every inquiry must fix in advance. Each procedure below is an imported candidate outline; its checks describe the intended outcome rather than an observed result of this revision.

<a id="t43"></a>
## T43: Estimate of one's competence

**Candidate improvement:** Competence estimates reflect representative performance at the intended support level.

**Procedure outline:** Define the skill and task conditions; inspect a representative performance; distinguish independent skill from assistance and favorable circumstances; update confidence only within the demonstrated range.

**Different recipes:** [A46a: Use representative mastery evidence](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a46a); [A60c: Support learning without replacing it](../attempts/decision-and-action.md#a60c); [A100c: Check unfamiliar application](../attempts/optimization-and-evaluation.md#a100c)

**Outcome check:** The estimate predicts future comparable performance better. If only assisted output improves, report increased supported capability rather than independent mastery.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S11](../sources.md#s11), [S21](../sources.md#s21)

## T44: Sense of agency

**Candidate improvement:** Agency tracks actual controllable transitions and available support.

**Procedure outline:** Separate personal actions from external outcomes; identify a meaningful controllable step; supply its missing prerequisite where feasible; connect completion to its real consequence.

**Different recipes:** [A44a: Bound personal control](../attempts/motivation-and-value.md#a44a); [A55a: Specify the first physical operation](../attempts/decision-and-action.md#a55a); [A29c: Change available resources](../attempts/concepts-and-possibilities.md#a29c)

**Outcome check:** The person can perform or initiate a consequential action. If the environment blocks all relevant action, change resources or scope rather than attributing helplessness to attitude.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.

<a id="t45"></a>
## T45: Sense of belonging

**Candidate improvement:** Belonging is supported by real participation opportunities and a more accurate interpretation of ambiguous setbacks.

**Procedure outline:** Inspect whether meaningful participation is actually available; connect the person to a concrete opportunity or relationship; distinguish an ambiguous setback from evidence of exclusion; evaluate the resulting experience without assigning blame for structural barriers.

**Different recipes:** [A74b: Change the social conditions](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a74b); [A75a: Separate event from appraisal](../attempts/conditions-and-appraisal.md#a75a); [A38c: Add a neglected vantage point](../attempts/trust-and-social-relations.md#a38c)

**Outcome check:** Participation and the person's own experience support the intended change. If opportunities are absent, alter the environment; reassurance alone is an inadequate route.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S18](../sources.md#s18)

## T46: Identity commitments

**Candidate improvement:** Identity commitments can be maintained, revised, or replaced without making one local belief immune to evidence.

**Procedure outline:** Identify the chosen commitment; separate it from a contingent factual claim or method; inspect the claim and its actual implications; preserve the commitment where compatible or make the real trade-off explicit.

**Different recipes:** [A36a: Separate identity from one claim](../attempts/trust-and-social-relations.md#a36a); [A36b: Preserve an end, change its means](../attempts/trust-and-social-relations.md#a36b); [A36c: Resolve competing commitments](../attempts/trust-and-social-relations.md#a36c)

**Outcome check:** The person can revise the local issue without being pressured into a prescribed identity. If the dependency is genuine, acknowledge rather than conceal it.

**Ingredient evidence:** No source assigned to this outline.

<a id="t47"></a>
## T47: Beliefs about social norms

**Candidate improvement:** Beliefs about others' behavior and approval reflect sourced, relevant social information.

**Procedure outline:** Define the relevant population; distinguish measured behavior from endorsement; compare the evidence with the current estimate; prevent the descriptive average from becoming an unintended target.

**Different recipes:** [A39a: Correct an overestimate of undesirable behavior](../attempts/trust-and-social-relations.md#a39a); [A39b: Protect behavior already better than average](../attempts/trust-and-social-relations.md#a39b); [A39c: Distinguish prevalence from endorsement](../attempts/trust-and-social-relations.md#a39c)

**Outcome check:** The norm estimate becomes more accurate and does not encourage regression from better behavior. If local evidence is missing, leave prevalence unknown.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S3](../sources.md#s3)

<a id="t48"></a>
## T48: Expected approval or rejection

**Candidate improvement:** Expected approval or rejection reflects the actual people, context, and available evidence.

**Procedure outline:** Identify whose response matters; separate direct evidence from an assumed general norm; consider the person's actual relationship and setting; choose a bounded interaction or interpretation supported by that evidence.

**Different recipes:** [A39c: Distinguish prevalence from endorsement](../attempts/trust-and-social-relations.md#a39c); [A38c: Add a neglected vantage point](../attempts/trust-and-social-relations.md#a38c); [A37b: Rehearse privately first](../attempts/trust-and-social-relations.md#a37b)

**Outcome check:** Predictions become more differentiated and, where observable, more accurate. If acceptance opportunities are genuinely absent, address the setting rather than promising approval.

**Ingredient evidence:** [S18](../sources.md#s18)
