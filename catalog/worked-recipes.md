# Completed recipes with supplied inputs and actual results

These procedures are complete for their declared finite inputs. They do not promise that an unspecified person will adopt their conclusions. Each result includes its derivation; [the implementation](../tools/deductions.py) and [actual outputs](../revision/deduction-results.json) make the computational cases inspectable.

| Recipe and eligibility | Operations actually performed | Result and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Choose an adequate action when possible situations and adequate actions are supplied | Intersect adequate-action sets; if empty, partition by available questions; solve every branch; select minimum worst-case cost | The three-state example needs one binary question, costing 1; full identity costs 3 and is unnecessary. No common action and no useful question returns an unresolved witness. [Derivation](../findings/recipe-adequacy.md). |
| Correct a policy under uncertain binary signal structure | Construct all compatible joint tables parametrically; calculate six policy payoffs; optimize endpoint regret over pure policies and pairs | At p=1/2, accuracy=4/5, cost=1, half immediate and half positive has regret 1/10. Accuracy zero makes the negative-response policy uniquely optimal. [Derivation](../findings/information-and-action.md). |
| Preserve answers while changing representation | State the relevant queries; check whether any merged objects have different answers; retain the distinction or reconstruction needed by those queries | The eight-node chain preserves reachability but changes 21 distances. The same summary is adequate for one question family and inadequate for another. [Derivation](../findings/organization-and-representation.md). |
| Repair a specified mixed failure mechanism | Evaluate all subsets of removable faults; retain exactly those that restore success and contain no smaller successful subset | For failure `(a AND b) OR (a AND c)`, minimal repairs are `{a}` and `{b,c}`. Fixing b alone fails. [Derivation](../findings/composition-and-failure.md). |
| Schedule the declared two-task deadline case with switch cost | Fix B in its forced window [1,3]; allocate A before and after it; include both switching intervals; derive a matching total-time lower bound | Every switch cost from 0 through 1/2 is feasible; larger costs are infeasible in this model. Starting the switch before B is released is essential at positive cost. [Derivation](../findings/time-effort-and-value.md). |
| Classify every starting state of a synchronous neighbor-XOR ring | Express the update as I+S; use characteristic-two powers and polynomial divisibility; independently enumerate small widths | Universal extinction holds exactly for power-of-two widths, with a tight n-step bound. This is a mathematical exploration, not a productivity intervention. [Derivation](../findings/neighbor-xor.md). |
| Edit original targets despite reorder and content changes | Resolve unique identities in one snapshot; check expected content before mutation; build the changed snapshot only after every precondition passes | Original A is removed and B tagged after reorder; a B-content mismatch leaves all input unchanged. Concurrent stores additionally need atomic commit enforcement. [Derivation](../findings/reference-and-concurrency.md). |
| Maintain independent restartable timers | Keep deadlines by round; ignore duplicate READY within an active round; cancel only its named round; permit a fresh READY after cancellation | Canceling A leaves B due at 9; restarting A at 10 sets A due at 15. [Derivation](../findings/reference-and-concurrency.md). |
| Recompute evidence when alarm dependence is unspecified | Bound each joint-high probability by its conditional marginals; substitute into Bayes' formula; evaluate monotone endpoints | Exact posterior range [1/4,1]; replacement occurs iff u>9v. In the specified partial-copy model the threshold is λ<7/32. [Derivation](../findings/dependence-and-changed-models.md). |
| Find a least-cost route with supplied XOR controls | Enumerate control subsets; compute their displacements and nonnegative costs; retain the cheapest matching subset | With A=110, B=011, C=101 and costs 1,1,3, choose AB at cost 2. If C costs 1, choose C. [Derivation](../findings/dependence-and-changed-models.md). |

The broader catalog remains useful for locating human intervention candidates and their ingredients. These completed recipes demonstrate the stronger standard: a substantive result replaces an unresolved central instruction when the premises make that possible. They are not presented as empirical validation of all 300 variants.
