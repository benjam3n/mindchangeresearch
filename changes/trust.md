# Trust

## Domain reliability

Sincerity, access, competence, permission, and reliability answer different questions about relying on a source or actor.

1. Name the specific claim or action on which reliance is proposed.
2. Identify the source's access to its evidence and competence for that particular task.
3. Separate repeated reports of one source from independent corroboration.
4. Retain uncertainty where the relevant reliability evidence is absent; do not fill it with general prestige or warmth.

Two dashboard fields can agree because they copy one event. Agreement among displays does not increase the independence of their evidence. A second genuinely independent sensor changes the evidential relation, not merely the number of reassuring messages.

The event-dependence example is exact within its supplied model. This procedure does not assign a general trustworthiness score to a person.
