| Property | Description |
|---|---|
| Condition | A specified claim is carrying a conclusion or plan, while its consequences or failure cases remain unclear. |
| Operation | Treat the claim as a conditional organizing assumption. Fix its scope; explore consequences if it holds; construct distinct ways it could fail; derive consequences of those alternatives; identify an observation or argument separating consequential branches. Record added assumptions. |
| Subject | Claim content specification, Claim inference counterexample, and Claim support limits |
| Result | a branch map, a relevant countercase, or a specified unresolved dependency. |
| Applicability | A counterexample requires the specified premises to hold while the conclusion fails. |
