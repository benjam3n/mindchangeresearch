| Perspective | Interpretation | Operation |
|---|---|---|
| Explanation cause | An outcome produced through some process | Construct and compare causal accounts that could produce the observed difference |
| Explanation contrast | A difference between what happened and a specified alternative | Identify what explains that particular contrast |
| Explanation alternative | An observation compatible with multiple accounts | Construct accounts that disagree about a further observation or intervention |
| Explanation discrimination | Competing accounts needing a separating result | Find an accessible observation with different implications for the accounts |
