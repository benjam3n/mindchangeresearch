| Perspective | Interpretation | Operation |
|---|---|---|
| Learning attribution | A performance change with an uncertain source | Distinguish the contributions of new knowledge, method, cue, resource, and changed task |
| Learning retention | A useful change that may be unavailable later | Preserve its content, retrieval conditions, and application limits |
| Learning transfer | A learned contribution encountering a new case | Identify what structure must remain and what can vary |
