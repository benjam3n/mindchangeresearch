| Perspective | Interpretation | Operation |
|---|---|---|
| Question presupposition | A question that assumes something unsettled | Examine its assumptions before treating its answer space as appropriate |
| Question answer space | A request whose possible satisfactory answers are unclear | Specify what an answer must distinguish and which answers are admissible |
| Question order | Questions whose usefulness depends on earlier answers | Arrange questions according to the information their successors require |
