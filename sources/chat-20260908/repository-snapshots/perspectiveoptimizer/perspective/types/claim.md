| Perspective | Interpretation | Operation |
|---|---|---|
| Claim specification | An assertion whose exact content is unclear | Fix its reference, quantifiers, scope, and truth conditions |
| Claim implication | An assumption whose consequences are not yet understood | Derive conditional consequences while retaining their premises |
| Claim failure | A proposition or inference that may fail in identifiable cases | Construct a relevant countercase or specify the limits of the search |
| Claim support | A conclusion whose grounds need examination | Identify evidence, dependence, defeaters, and what acceptance is justified |
