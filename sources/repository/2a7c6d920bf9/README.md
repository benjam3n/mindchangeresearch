# Mind change research

Develop useful, efficient ways to change what a mind can notice, understand, care about and do—including changes in the conditions, aims and standards through which those possibilities arise.

The starting account must be able to lose its authority without first being proved false. That requirement now governs a correction to the research itself; it also remains open to further examination.

The complete historical corpus is available through native repository paths. [Original conversation artifacts](sources/perspective-inquiries-25/README.md) are preserved with hashes. Their inquiry pages are retrieval views, not additional findings. Existing original-skill sources, receipts, rejected results and unfinished stages remain available.

A finite correct account can be useful within its scope while failing to constitute the inquiry now needed. The existing [recipe compiler](post-allocation/cycle-03/consolidation-03-recipe-compiler.md) remains a route for sufficiently specified transitions. It no longer supplies a mandatory entrance requirement for every inquiry.

The inquiry has not established an exhaustive set of all possibilities or achieved and certified perfected understanding. It preserves the ambition and identifies those unmet obligations. Counts, source fidelity, local demonstrations, recipient effects and durable change remain different claims.
