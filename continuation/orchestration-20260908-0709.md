## Error recovery

Duplicate work: detect matching slot/input hashes; retain the first evidence chain and mark repeats as reuse or rejection, never another exposure. Retry limit: one correction after identifying a concrete defect.

Invalid dependency: detect missing or semantically invalid parent; withdraw dependent depth and reopen from a valid parent. Preserve the rejected edge.

Generated-but-unreviewed record: keep outside the common ledger until semantic review. Rollback is exclusion, not deletion.

Unavailable human or external data: stop that stage, mark the exact requirement partial or blocked, and continue other ready work. No automatic substitution.

Write/version conflict: retain the authoritative remote version and produce a separate reconciliation record. Never remove the version guard.
