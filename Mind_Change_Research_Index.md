The [25 inquiries](inquiries/perspective-25/README.md), [616 target and contributor entries](mind-change/targets.md), and [Subject Systems](mind-change/subject-systems.md) remain available alongside these twenty procedures with concrete sequences, cases, and stated evidence. This is partial coverage, and the statement rankings do not establish comparative recipe efficacy.

| Subject | Procedure | Distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Understanding | [Contrasting cases](changes/understanding.md#contrasting-cases) | Knowing a rule includes distinguishing a case it governs from a near match it does not govern. Repeating its wording leaves that distinction unexamined. |
| Understanding | [Independent reconstruction](changes/understanding.md#independent-reconstruction) | A supplied answer and the ability to produce it under changed conditions are different capacities. The missing operation becomes visible when the conclusion is withheld. |
| Evidence | [Event provenance](changes/evidence.md#event-provenance) | Two reports copied from one event supply one event's evidence. Two different authors do not establish two independent observations. |
| Evidence | [Discriminating observations](changes/evidence.md#discriminating-observations) | An observation that every remaining explanation predicts cannot separate those explanations. Additional reports can leave the actual uncertainty untouched. |
| Attention | [Attention selection](changes/attention.md#attention-selection) | A visible item and an item governing the next operation are different. Keeping all material in the record does not require processing all of it at once. |
| Memory | [Conditional indexing](changes/memory.md#conditional-indexing) | Stored information remains unusable when the encountered condition does not lead to its retrieval. An index changes access while leaving the underlying information intact. |
| Memory | [Conditional transfer](changes/memory.md#conditional-transfer) | A remembered action can transfer while its justification does not. The retained condition determines whether reuse is warranted. |
| Action | [Prerequisite order](changes/action.md#prerequisite-order) | Possessing every required component does not make an action executable when its prerequisites arrive too late. |
| Action | [Starting and stopping](changes/action.md#starting-and-stopping) | Initiation, continuation, interruption, and termination are different controls. A strong intention supplies none of their execution receipts by itself. |
| Motivation | [Desire and method](changes/motivation.md#desire-and-method) | Rejecting a method does not establish rejection of its goal. Wanting the goal does not establish an ability to execute the method. |
| Emotion | [Feeling and interpretation](changes/emotion.md#feeling-and-interpretation) | A reported feeling supplies evidence of that report. It does not establish every causal or factual interpretation attached to the feeling. |
| Emotion | [Adverse conditions](changes/emotion.md#adverse-conditions) | An unwanted experience can disclose a condition that should change. Reinterpreting the experience cannot be assumed to resolve the condition. |
| Goals | [Method requirements](changes/goals.md#method-requirements) | A condition required by one method becomes a requirement of the goal only if every adequate route requires it. |
| Goals | [Success criteria](changes/goals.md#success-criteria) | A procedure can satisfy its recorded target while failing the concern that made the target worthwhile. |
| Disagreement | [Claim comparison](changes/disagreement.md#claim-comparison) | Identical facts can support different priorities; identical words can express different claims. The disagreement is not identified until the conflicting commitment is located. |
| Trust | [Domain reliability](changes/trust.md#domain-reliability) | Sincerity, access, competence, permission, and reliability answer different questions about relying on a source or actor. |
| Perception | [Query and representation](changes/perception.md#query-and-representation) | Two representations can preserve the same facts while exposing different relations. The question determines which loss matters. |
| Imagination | [Alternative interpretations](changes/imagination.md#alternative-interpretations) | A current interpretation can be correct within its own question and still exclude a more useful question. Its failure need not be established before an alternative can be developed. |
| Values | [Separate value conditions](changes/values.md#separate-value-conditions) | An apparent tradeoff can be produced by bundling together fields that serve different concerns. Separating the fields can change the available alternatives. |
| Feedback | [Correction timing](changes/feedback.md#correction-timing) | A correct report can arrive after the action it could have changed is no longer available. Information quality and remaining correction opportunity are separate. |
