## Candidate and concrete interpretation checks

Timer policy — one clock domain, five seconds. When inactive, the first `READY` starts a round: store `ready_at` and set `deadline=ready_at+5`. While active, ignore later `READY` events as duplicates. `CANCEL` clears the active round; the next `READY` may start a new one. At a poll, report `remaining=max(0,deadline−now)` and `due=(now≥deadline)`. “Due” describes state at the poll, not guaranteed delivery at the deadline.

Checks: `READY 3, READY 6, POLL 8` keeps deadline 8 and is due. `READY 3, CANCEL 4, READY 10` gives deadline 15: one second remains at poll 14 and it is due at 15. Held out: `READY 11, READY 13` gives one second at 15 and due at 16.

Interpretations: display anchoring would give deadline 5 in the first trace; restart-on-every-READY would give 11; session-global first-READY would reject the legitimate READY at 10. The candidate selects 8, then 15, by naming both anchor and active-round scope. A poll at 10 for deadline 8 reports due at 10; it does not establish delivery at 8.
