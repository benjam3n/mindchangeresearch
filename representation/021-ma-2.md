Cross-dimension interactions:

1. One-line extraction × footnote limitations loses the condition unless the first field already carries it.
2. Benefit-descending order × disqualifying first field separates a veto from a low score: an inapplicable option is excluded rather than merely ranked lower.
3. Actor grouping × required action identifies who must do the next step; the label does not establish consent or performance.
4. Symbol cue × exact words can survive plain text; symbol alone cannot distinguish unknown from rejected reliably without its legend.
5. Unresolved trade-off × single chosen option cannot both be the sole exit; they are distinct exit states, not coequal decorations.
6. Least-burden order × prerequisite-first order can conflict; a necessary input stays before its dependent operation even when costly.
