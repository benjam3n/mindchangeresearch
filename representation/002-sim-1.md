The component map retains every decision-bearing element. Essential: current request/context/capability; actor and locus; credible ordinary continuation; alternative changing the locus when the live choice is consequential; fit between operation and actual input; prerequisites and result-dependent order; benefits and total costs; authority to perform; actual execution; scoped consequence; revision or stopping; retained trigger and boundary. Supporting: the long enumeration of possible dimensions, examples of inferred purposes, and examples of costs. Decorative: none in the source is safely classified as mere style; the source is already functional. The simplification therefore removes examples from the working view but preserves their link, rather than pretending that a substantial cut proves superiority.

A first reconstruction was: “Choose a change, perform it, compare its result, retain what works.” Its missing conditions were tested directly. A relocation requiring the user’s action was falsely expressible as “perform it”; a sourced but untested claim was expressible as “works”; an expensive diagnostic was not constrained by total burden; a beneficial unchanged state disappeared. That reconstruction is rejected.

Corrected working sequence:

1. Recover the live concern, context, relevant prior correction, and available authority. Keep person, model, and shared conditions distinct.
2. Compare the strongest current continuation with a materially different viable change when that choice matters. Preserve a useful state when changing it adds no supported benefit.
3. Choose the operation and its locus from actual input, benefits, total burden, prerequisites, and uncertainty. Obtain a discriminating observation when its value warrants the cost; otherwise make a bounded reversible choice.
4. Perform the authorized change. A proposed human action remains a proposal until performed.
5. Compare what occurred with the intended effect and relevant alternative. Attribute reports and preserve competing explanations and unknown transfer.
6. Continue, repair, redirect, defer with a real continuation mechanism, or stop. Retain the situation, operation, evidence, scope, exception, and next-use trigger; check them at a suitable recurrence.
