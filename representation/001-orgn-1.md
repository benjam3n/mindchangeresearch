Two viable structures were compared. A single centralized result queue gives the root one place to review all evidence and exceptions; it also forces local reader decisions through material the root has not yet integrated. A local evidence-qualified handoff gives the root scoped findings and links while retaining its authority. Local saving by every worker would duplicate a root-owned process and is outside this assignment. The adopted structure is the evidence-qualified handoff, with raw execution retained locally.

The new routing card is concrete:

1. Artifact or model decision observed in this line → local record, exact input and result, eligible for a local KEEP.
2. Human design without use → proposed intervention, UNRESOLVED human effect.
3. Report by another actor → attribute it and preserve its scope; no independent-evidence upgrade.
4. Source checksum → provenance evidence only.
5. Consolidation or publication → root handoff, never implied by local existence.
6. New contradiction within a KEEP’s scope → reopen that finding; outside its scope → preserve the old result and open a transfer question.
