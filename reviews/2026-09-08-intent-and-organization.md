# Where the research misread the request, and what changes

Intended mind change: Replace an execution-and-accounting interpretation of the research with an inquiry that produces useful changes while allowing its own targets, units and judges to change.

Actual starting judgment: The existing program was already broadly about mind change, and its latest scoped recipe compiler could provide the governing entry procedure for future work. The second part of that judgment does not survive this review. The compiler is useful for declared transitions whose terms are sufficiently settled; treating it as the entrance to all inquiry would exclude several changes the user explicitly requests.

Evidence base: the review began from repository commit `07e6af64c65fb682305659b2ec830e21743c0f15`, its verified complete checkpoint, the user's 25 inquiries, and the delivered investigation imported with byte hashes. [Historical controls](../sources/repository-review-2026-09-08/README-source.md) preserve the exact reviewed versions. This is an interpretation of visible instructions and artifacts, not a claim about the user's private intentions or unobserved effects.

## The central substitution

The research often answered: “Given an identifiable target and supported criterion, how can a procedure produce and certify a change?” The current request also asks: “What if identifying that target and criterion already preserves the inadequacy?” A procedure can be rigorous about the first question and systematically miss the second.

The old working instruction required specifying “state variable, locus, criterion, constraints, transition operator, preserved invariant, and shape-specific success witness” before selecting a mechanism. The local [cycle-03 consolidation](../post-allocation/cycle-03/consolidation-03-recipe-compiler.md) gave a reason for that discipline in its six cases. Promoting it to a universal entrance requirement gave the predecessor account authority over the arrival of its successor. More meticulous compliance would deepen that particular misreading.

## What this correction must not repeat

“Make everything revisable” can itself become a universal admission test. It would then exclude a development that does not understand itself as revision, or force every experience into an account of governance. The imported inquiries place that account at risk too. These pages give present routes and arguments; they do not require every future understanding to justify itself in these terms before it can be developed.

Likewise, a mode menu is not a new universal order. A practical access request may be appropriate immediately; an encounter may precede any classified aim; a proof may legitimately keep its terms fixed. Route selection is itself open when the case unsettles the terms that would select it.

The thesis that every problem involves inadequate perspective does not entail that perspective is the sole cause of every adverse condition, that an available change is sufficient to remove it, or that a text model can enact every relevant intervention. The model-capacity and perfection assumptions are developed as assumptions. Their truth, practical access and certification remain distinct.

## Organization adopted and actual use

Three candidate organizations were compared through concrete retrieval tasks. A chronological skill ledger finds provenance and outstanding obligations well but requires the reader to infer the practical route. A universal compiler gives a short consistent interface but prejudges whether the target and criterion are settled. Multiple entrances—change sought or emerging, executable route, foundational inquiry, source/evidence, and progress—retain those uses without giving one of them universal authority.

The third organization is implemented. The [integration record](../studies/2026-09-08-perspective-integration.md) shows actual query-to-record retrievals and countercases. It does not establish an optimal organization or measure user comprehension. The new pages themselves can be replaced as the work exposes better divisions.

## Remaining work made concrete

1. Apply the new routes to a genuinely new encounter, preserving its credible starting interpretation. Where another person's experience is the subject, obtain that person's account and relevant observed consequences instead of manufacturing them.
2. Develop stronger operations for affect, interest, perceptual availability and embodied conditions. Use the relevant original procedures where selected; obtain primary evidence for empirical claims. The present index makes these live research gaps, not settled impossibilities.
3. Compare a route with its strongest credible alternative on both achieved change and the effort required, including setup and later use. A recipe can fail despite a completed procedure.
4. Test an inquiry in which the unit, candidate set or criterion actually changes. Report old and new interpretations and the remaining disagreement. Do not retrofit it as an ordinary score improvement.
5. Continue the frozen original obligations when their missing inputs are available, and additional substantive work when those gates remain closed. These streams serve the ambition without defining it.

Actual mind change: The finite compiler has lost its universal governing role. Source fidelity, exact local reasoning and outstanding obligations remain intact. The repository now allows work whose subject, goal and standards are among what may change.

Benefit: Current readers can directly reach practical routes, their limitations and the new foundational inquiries; future checkpoint generation preserves those entry points. Human benefit, general efficiency gains and perfected understanding remain unestablished.

Verdict: Adopt this repository repair. This is a maintenance and interpretive correction, not an additional counted KEEP discovery or certification of all historical records.

Organization: Multiple revisable entrances, exact historical sources, independent progress accounting.

Next attempts: The five concrete tasks above, with particular priority to achieved changes outside symbolic correction and to encounters that change the terms of inquiry.
