# What this research is trying to achieve

Find and achieve beneficial, efficient changes across what a mind can notice, understand, care about, remember, imagine and do, including changes in the conditions through which those activities become possible. Produce usable ways of bringing such changes about. Let the inquiry change its understanding of benefit, efficiency, mind, change and its own ambition.

The user's [25 inquiries](sources/perspective-inquiries-25/user-request.md) make an especially demanding requirement explicit: **the starting account must be able to lose its governing authority without first being proved false.** Research that only improves answers to questions it has already authorized does not satisfy that requirement.

This is a working interpretation of the request, open to correction by the user and the work. It does not claim privileged access to what the user ultimately wants.

## What belongs within the ambition

- Actual changes in attention, perceptual availability, interpretation, representation, confidence, feeling, interest, motivation, values, identity, memory, skill, conduct, time, pace, location, resources, coordination and relations with other people.
- Changes in what becomes a concern, what counts as one subject, what alternatives can be formed, what makes an account intelligible, and who or what is granted authority to judge it.
- Enabling or constitutive changes in body, tools, surroundings and shared practice, where relevant. Their relation to mind is an inquiry, not settled by putting them in either a mental or an external box.
- Recipes with concrete operations, sufficient conditions where established, actual worked uses, and visible failure boundaries. An instruction to select the right mechanism is an outstanding design task, not that mechanism.
- The possibility of perfected understanding as a serious ambition. Neither a finite catalogue nor a standing promise to remain open establishes perfection. Existence, reachability, recognition and certification are different obligations.

## What the existing program gets right

The corpus already extends well beyond changing beliefs. Its finite cases preserve useful distinctions between evidence and copies, timing and option expiry, information and access, preparation and execution, and model output and recipient effect. Its source receipts and unfinished stages protect against invented completion. These accomplishments survive the correction to their jurisdiction.

The frozen allocation also survives: 150 original Reasoningtool skills, with 50×3 + 50×2 + 50×1 = 300 applications, plus ten GOSM runs. Four distinct, demonstrated, unconsolidated KEEP findings trigger a consolidation. These are obligations within the program. They do not define beneficial mind change or decide which questions can arise.

## How to pursue the ambition without shrinking it

Permit the formation of a new way of understanding before demanding an agreed target, a shared metric or a translation into the old account. Exploration, endorsement, action and a claim of success require different kinds of support. Their distinction is not a compulsory sequence; an encounter or an action can itself create the terms later used to understand it.

For a declared finite comparison, state its input, criterion and scope so the comparison means something. If the inquiry changes those terms, retain the old comparison as an answer to its old question and investigate the new question. A change in criteria cannot be reported as a score improvement under unchanged criteria. It also need not pass those unchanged criteria to become thinkable.

Keep practical limits exact. A text response cannot by itself establish a bodily intervention, durable learning or another person's agreement. That limitation does not excuse failing to design the best available route, identify what would enable it, or explore changes that current representations obscure.

The [change index](changes/README.md), [recipes](recipes/README.md), [inquiries](inquiries/perspective-25/README.md), and [progress record](progress.md) offer different entrances to the same corpus. Their boundaries are revisable; none is an exhaustive partition of minds or inquiry.

The concurrently accepted contributor inventory and its standing instruction to use relevant [Subject Systems](mind-change/subject-systems.md) for identified assistant weaknesses remain operative. They provide substantive operations and coverage; the 25 inquiries can also put their subjects, systems and organizing categories into question without bypassing actual user instructions.
